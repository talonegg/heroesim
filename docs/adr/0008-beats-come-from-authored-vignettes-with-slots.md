# Beats come from authored Vignettes with slots

Beats are neither hand-written one by one nor assembled procedurally from parts. They come from
authored Vignettes: templates carrying slots that the engine fills from world state — this Hero,
this Trait, this Patron's Sphere — and conditions saying when they may fire.

The reasoning is that in this game the story quality comes from specificity to the people
involved, not from novelty of situation. "Brenna, who is Craven, freezes at the bridge" reads
well even when that bridge has appeared before, because Brenna is the player's. Fully authored
content runs dry within a weekend of play; fully procedural content produces prose nobody reads
twice.

## Consequences

Vignettes are content, not code: adding one is writing a file, never changing the engine. The
engine's job is matching conditions and filling slots, and it should stay that small.
