# Traits are the universal currency of character

A Background, an Injury, a Scar and an innate disposition are all modelled as Traits rather than
as four parallel systems. An Injury is a Trait carrying a span of Days after which it lifts; a
Scar is one that never does; a Background is simply the Traits someone started with. The payoff
is that a person's whole history is readable as the Traits they have accrued, and one mechanism
for "a named fact that conditionally modifies a check" serves all of it.

The cost is that Traits carry a small amount of per-kind structure rather than each kind getting
a bespoke type. That is deliberate: a future reader tempted to split out an `injuries` field
should know it was considered and rejected.
