# The Guildmaster never joins a Party

The Guildmaster has Stats and Traits like anyone else, so the obvious affordance is to let the
player send themselves on a Commission. They cannot. Their Stats resolve Guild-side checks —
negotiating a fee, recruiting, talking round a Hero whose Regard is sliding — and they shape what
a Party can do through its Backing, but they never leave the hall.

A Guildmaster who can join a Party is immediately the strongest piece on the board, and the
Roster becomes a supporting cast to the player's own character — which inverts the game. Keeping
them home also leaves insolvency as the sole way to lose, rather than adding a competing "you
personally died" ending.
