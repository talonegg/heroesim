# v1.2.4 — The starting economics baseline

**Companion to:** [the v1.2.4 epic](./v1.2.4-release.md) · **Read at:** `65c2465`, 2026-10-17, config
version 39 · **Measured figures:** `v1.2.3`'s closing Gate, as
[gate-outcomes-monitoring.md](gate-outcomes-monitoring.md) records it

Every income and expense a Guild has before the economy pass changes anything: its rule, the settings
and content rows it reads, and a range of what it comes to. A snapshot for S7 to read the levers from,
not a live page: the rules' homes are `engine/src/config.json`, `content/` and the engine files named.
The pass tunes not from these values but from the owner's revised starting point,
[the epic's §2a](./v1.2.4-release.md#2a-the-starting-point) (DL-328).

## Units

- **Wealth** is held in whole Silver; 100 Silver is a Gold (DL-294, DL-296). Ranges below are written
  `5g 20s`, the CLI's form.
- **A Year** is 360 Days (`turnsPerYear` 1,080 ÷ 3). The wages fall due every 10 Days
  (`upkeepEveryDays`), 36 charges a Year; the Hall's upkeep every 30 (`hallUpkeepEveryDays`), 12 a Year.
- **Negotiation**, a Guildmaster Stat (1 to 20), reaches ±10% (`negotiationRange` 0.1,
  `engine/src/guildmaster.ts`): a Quest's reward × (1 + n); a signing fee, a Transfer fee and a wage ×
  (1 − n). It does not touch Market prices or Improvement costs.

### Endings, not charges

- **Bankruptcy:** Wealth below `bankruptcyFloor` 0 for `bankruptcyGraceDays` 30 ends the Guild. The
  player's game ends; a Rival folds, its Unique Items to the Market and its stacks discarded.
- **A Rival's debt sale** (`debtTransfer`, DL-301) is built but never reached in play, since the
  releases come first (DL-322); open as Q-M and Q-O in the epic's §0.

## D. The levers the pass holds

- **S10, the player's solvency:** the wage's keys, the Hall's and each Facility's upkeep, the reward by
  Tier, the signing fee's keys, the policy's reserves.
- **S11, the Rivals' living:** each Disposition's thresholds, `rivalHireReserveUpkeeps` and the Rivals'
  starting Wealth.
- **S12, the Market:** `marketSize`.
- **S13, the Contracts' figures:** `transferFeeFactor`, `releasePayoutFactor` and the free Offer's Days.
- **Held at 0:** `wageFameFactor`, `wageBase` and the Transfer premium.

## Assumptions

- The wage and fee ranges take a Hero's Stat sum, 20 Stats, as about 100 to 300; the Heroes
  Generator's spread was not read. It barely moves a wage, and moves a signing fee by about ±25s.
- An Encounter's `wealth` factors multiply, so one Quest's reward can fall outside A1's ranges.

## E. The estimate model

[v1.2.4-economics-model.html](./v1.2.4-economics-model.html), one page with no dependencies, opened
in a browser from the repository or as the owner's private claude.ai artifact
(<https://claude.ai/artifact/AUebEKcwK25oemYTznFJ8E>), published from this file without its
`<html>`, `<head>` and `<body>` tags, which claude.ai adds. It estimates section C, a Guild-year's balance, from sections A and
B's ranges and a handful of gameplay assumptions, so that the owner can move a range and see the rough
size of its effect before a trial is run. A rough-order estimate for rebaselining, not a simulation:
the harness's Gate remains the measure.

### What it is for

- *Rebaselining the costs.* Change an item's min or max, or an assumption, and read each line of C
  move against the preset's defaults (Impact) and against the `v1.2.3` close (Measured).
- *Ranking the levers* for S9 to S13 before their full `gate`s: which settings move the balance by
  Gold a Year and which by Silver.

### The rules

Every range is read at one position `p`: its min, its middle (Mid) or its max; `u(x)` is item `x` at
`p`. A Year is 36 wage charges and 12 Hall charges.

| Line of C | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Quest rewards | Quests × (1 − failed) × Σ (Tier share × (`u(Tier reward)` + `u(its Encounter swing)`)) × (1 + Negotiation) × reward realised |
| Item sales | Σ sold a Year × ⌊`u(price)` × `u(sell ratio)`⌋, for Consumables and Common gear (B9's prices) and Unique Items (their value) |
| Transfer fees received | Heroes sold × ⌊`u(charges left)` × mean wage × `u(transferFeeFactor)`⌋, the mean wage Σ (Heroes at a Level × `u(wage at that Level)`) ÷ Roster |
| Wages | Σ (Heroes at a Level × `u(wage at that Level)`) × 36 × (1 + Out of Contract share × `u(B1a)`) |
| The Hall's upkeep | (`u(B2)` + `u(B3)` × Roster) × 12 × the Guildmaster's factor |
| The Improvements' upkeep | Σ (Facilities at a grade × `u(B4 at that grade)`) × 12 × the Guildmaster's factor |
| Items bought | Σ bought a Year × `u(B9)` |
| Improvements built | Σ built a Year × `u(B8 at that grade)` |
| Signing fees | Σ signed a Year at a Level × `u(B6 at that Level)` |
| Transfer fees paid; release payouts | As Transfer fees received: Heroes bought at `transferFeeFactor`; Heroes released at `releasePayoutFactor` |
| The balance | Income in all − expenses in all |
| One Upkeep | (Wages + the Hall's + the Improvements' upkeep) ÷ 12, a month of costs (DL-300) |
| Wealth after N Years | `u(A5)` + N × the balance |

**Outputs.** Each line at Min, Mid and Max. The balance's Min is every income at its min against every
expense at its max, and its Max the reverse, so they bound the worst and best case. Impact is Mid now
against Mid at the preset's defaults; Measured is the `v1.2.3` close's line; a footer gives the
Roster and the balance in months of Upkeep. Edits are kept in the browser that made them, and *Reset
to the preset* restores the defaults.

**Locks.** Each input has a padlock. A locked field keeps its value when a preset is picked or reset,
so a change can be carried across the scenarios; *Unlock all* clears every lock. Impact still reads
against the preset's own defaults, so a locked change shows as its effect on each preset.

**Export.** *Export to Markdown* writes the model as it stands to
`v1.2.4-economics-model-<YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS>.md`: section C, the Roster and months line, every
assumption and every item range (A1's Encounter columns, and each derived row's computed min and
max), each with its default and its *Changed* and *Locked* marks. On claude.ai it is
saved through the artifact's download prompt (the `downloads` capability), to be moved here. Opened
from the repository, Chrome and Edge save it through their Save dialog, which reopens in the folder
last chosen, so pick this folder once; other browsers download it, to be moved here.

### What it leaves out

- *Time.* One steady Guild-year: no Roster growth, Levelling, Tier unlocks, Contract ends or Fame
  within it. The close's Wealth rises then falls over a game, which a mean Guild-year hides, so the
  harness's Wealth in months of Upkeep (13.17) is not reproduced; §F's scenarios give five such years
  along the game.
- *Coupling.* The assumptions are independent: a cheaper wage does not draw more Heroes, nor does a
  larger Roster take more Quests or higher Tiers. Move the related assumptions together.
- *The sides.* The player's rewards are the Rivals' lost Quests, and the Rivals' Wealth sets the
  Transfer market; each preset is one side alone.
- *The Stat sum.* As in the Assumptions above.

### How each is estimated

- **The Roster** is the year-start's Heroes a Guild. Its wage a charge is 0.872 × the wage rule at the
  Roster's mean Level (`(20 × Level + 4) × 0.95`, a 2-year Contract at a middling Stat sum and
  Negotiation); the Heroes are placed between the Level buckets so that their wage mids (B1) give it.
  The 0.872 and no lag behind the Level were fitted, with the reward's factor, to the Wealth change of
  all 20 Years at once.
- **The Tier mix:** the Tier 3 share from the Year's Loot (Loot falls only on a strong Tier 3 or higher
  Quest: the Loot over Tier 3 and 4's strong Quests, 0.26, at Tier 3's strong share against the Year's);
  Tier 1 and 2 share the rest so that the mean difficulty meets the Year's, Tier 1's Quests at 19,
  Tier 2's at 37 and Tier 3's at 50. Summed over the 20 Years it gives each Tier's Quests within about
  3% of the close's. Year 1 is Tier 1 alone, the only Tier open. Failed is each Tier's failed share at the mix.
- **The Hall** is the infirmary at grade 3, built in Year 1 (the policy builds no other Facility). The
  Market's lines are the mean Guild-year's, which the report does not hold by Year; the signings are
  the 0.19 a Year that replace Heroes after Year 1, none at Day 100.
- **Year 1 Day 100** has the Roster full, which the policy reaches in about the first month (it signs
  while Wealth is over one Upkeep), and the infirmary built. Its Wealth, 4,643, is Year 1's change taken
  evenly to Day 100, and its projection runs the 0.72 Year to Year 2 Day 1.
- **Reward realised** is the scenario's calibration: fitted so that its Mid balance meets the measured
  change in Wealth to a Year on (Day 100's, to Year 2 Day 1). It carries what the estimate above leaves:
  across the 20 Years the fitted rule misses a Year's balance by about 2,900 (the root mean square).

### What to read with them

- *Survivors.* Wealth a Year on is the mean of the Guilds still playing then, so a Year in which
  Guilds go bankrupt reads better than its average Guild did (Year 15: 32 Guilds, 28 a Year on).
- *The lines but the balance* are estimates: of each scenario only its balance and its Wealth a Year on
  are measured.
