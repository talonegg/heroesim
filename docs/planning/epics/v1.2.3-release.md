# v1.2.3 — Contracts: a wage set at signing, a length chosen, and Heroes who leave when it ends

**Objective:** give every Hero in every Guild a Contract (DL-294 item 6). Its wage is set at signing
from the Hero's Level and Stats and fixed for its term, and the signer chooses its length, 1, 2 or 3
years, a longer term cheaper each period. A Guild may renew at any time, and may release a Hero with a
payout of most of the wages left. An expired Hero keeps playing, Out of Contract, at a wage repriced
with a premium (Q-D). Another Guild can take them for no fee unless their Guild renews within 3 Days,
and a Hero an Offer stands for is Under Offer, kept from every Party until it ends (Q-G, Q-H). The
Transfer fee becomes the wages left on the Contract. The Rivals and the harness's policy follow the
same rules (ADR-0002). Every new figure starts near `v1.2.2`'s cost (Q21), so the closing Gate shows
what the rules do, not new magnitudes.

**Aims at:** sentence 5 of the [design](../../management-design.md), "If I don't renew her Contract,
someone else will take her." Every Contract has an end the player can see coming. At expiry, a free
Offer the player can answer only by renewing.

The owner's answers to Q6–Q12 and Q15 of `v1.2.2`'s epic (its §6), given on 2026-10-12, set this
release's rules; DL-294 item 6 records them. The owner's answers to this epic's Q-A to Q-I (§6) settle
the rest; DL-311 records Q-D to Q-I and this plan's review against what `v1.2.2` delivered, and
DL-312 the owner's second word on Q-F.

### v1.2.3-S2 · The Contract and its wage · 3 sessions · opus, rows and page sonnet

- *The record* (Q6). A Hero on a Roster holds `contract`: the wage, the Turn signed, the length in
  years and the end Day; a Hero on no Roster holds none. Its end is the Day the term's years run out,
  a year's Days read as `turnsPerYear` ÷ `turnsPerDay` (`engine/src/config.json`, `time.ts`), never a
  constant. `schemaVersion` 14; `docs/data-model.md`. The validator checks a Roster Hero holds one and
  no other Hero does.
- *The wage* (Q6, Q7b), test first, one rule reading the whole game, the Hero, their Guild and its
  Roster, so the backlog's terms join inside it (DL-294 item 8): (`wageBase` + `wagePerLevel` ×
  Level + Stat sum ÷ `wageStatDivisor` + `wageFameFactor` × the Guild's Fame) × the length's factor × (1 −
  Negotiation), rounded down once, whole Silver. `contractLengthsYears` [1, 2, 3] and
  `contractWageFactors` [1, 0.95, 0.9] are two config lists of one length, checked by `checkConfig`.
  The new settings are added by hand (§0, Settings).
- *Charged.* `wages` (`engine/src/selectors.ts`) sums each living Roster Hero's Contract wage, so a
  Level gained no longer moves it, and `upkeep`, the month's measure, follows. `guild.wages-paid` names each Hero's wage, a map in `moneyMaps`
  (`phases/guild-state.ts`, `validate.ts`).
- *Signed* at every signing: `recruit` and the Transfer actions take the length the signer chooses
  (`engine/src/actions/recruit.ts`, `actions/transfer.ts`). In a Transfer the buyer chooses and the
  old Contract ends. `hero.recruited` and `hero.transferred` carry the terms, their wage in
  `moneyFields`, and their sentences say them (`words/src/narrate.ts`).
- *The page and the CLI sign with a length.* The Pool shows each Hero's signing fee beside their first
  wage at each length, three columns, and the length is chosen at signing (`web/src/views/pool.tsx`);
  the page's Transfers take one too (`views/guilds.tsx`, `hero-page.tsx`). The CLI's `recruit` and
  `transfer` take `--years`, and its usage says so (`cli/src/commands/`, `args.ts`).
- *Who chooses* (Q7c). A Disposition's `contractYears`, Steady 2, Ambitious 1, Miserly 3, Reckless 1,
  by the kind's own steps (`content/dispositions.json`, `content/src/kinds/dispositions.ts`); the
  policy 2 years, a harness setting (`harness/src/policy.ts`).
- *The founding Contracts* (Q7c, Q-E). Every founding Contract, the player's and the Rivals', has its
  length drawn evenly from the list and starts part-way through its term, its end Day drawn evenly
  over the term's Days, so a founding Roster's Contracts do not end together. Both draws come from a
  new stream, `contracts`, appended to `streamNames` (`engine/src/rng.ts`, ADR-0009), so no draw of
  `generation` moves (`engine/src/generators/guilds.ts`, `create.ts`). A Rival founded at a fold draws
  the same way.
- *Ended* by a Death, a Retirement, a Transfer, or a fold, whose Heroes join the pool with none
  (`phases/endings.ts`).

### v1.2.3-S3 · Renewal, and the Contract's end · 1.5 sessions · opus, stop and words sonnet

- *Renewal* (Q9). A new action, `renew`: the Hero's Guild signs them again at any time, at the wage
  rule's price for their Level and Stats now and a length it chooses, with no fee. The new term starts
  that Day and the old one's rest is dropped. Its refusals: a Hero not on the Roster, away on a Quest
  (the owner, §6 Q-A), dead or retired. `hero.contract-renewed`, its wage in `moneyFields`. The page
  and the CLI reach it at S9.
- *The end.* On the night of its end Day the Contract expires: `hero.contract-expired`, a Milestone
  for every Guild's Hero, the Rivals' too (`milestoneTypes`). The Hero stays on the Roster and plays,
  Out of Contract, until renewed, released or taken (S6). That night their wage is repriced (Q-D): the
  wage rule at the 1-year factor × (1 + `outOfContractPremium`), rounded down once, carried on the
  event and charged from then. `outOfContractPremium` is added by hand (§0, Settings). A stop,
  `contract-expired`, for the Watched Guild (`engine/src/stops.ts`), placed in this story in every map
keyed by stop kind: `--until`'s words and usage (`cli/src/args.ts`), `narrateStop`
(`words/src/narrate.ts`) and the stop card's (`web/src/views/stop.tsx`). No stop before the end; the
  Roster and the Hero page show the Days left (S9).

### v1.2.3-S4 · Release, with a payout · 1.5 sessions · sonnet

- *The wages left*, one selector, `wagesLeft` (`engine/src/selectors.ts`): the wage × the wage charges
  left before the end Day, 0 once expired. The release payout reads it, and the Transfer fee at S5.
- *Release* (Q10). A new action, `release`: during the term the Guild pays `releasePayoutFactor`
  (0.8) × `wagesLeft`; nothing once expired. The Hero joins the Unattached pool with no Contract,
  their gear returned to the Holding as a Transfer's is. Each teammate who held them warmly or better
  loses `transferMoraleCost`, as at a Transfer out (`engine/src/transfer.ts`). Refused for a Hero away
  on a Quest. `hero.released`, its payout in the payload and in `moneyFields`.
- *The last Hero* (the owner, Q-B). A Guild may release its last Hero, and the game warns first. One
  selector, `releaseWarning`, names the case: the last Hero, and whether the Wealth left after the
  payout covers the cheapest Hero in the pool (`cheapestUnattached`). The page's confirmation and the
  CLI's `--confirm` read it (S9). The engine refuses nothing for it: a Guild that cannot sign another
  Hero meets the "Roster wiped" ending, and for the player's Guild the game ends
  (`phases/endings.ts`). A Rival's or the policy's release follows the same rule, with no warning.
- *The ledger* (Q19). The payout is an expense line of its own in the Treasury
  (`web/src/state/treasury.ts`) and a `WealthFlows` counter in the harness (`harness/src/counters.ts`)
  from this story, so no payout is ever read as Other Expense.
- *The ending* "Roster wiped" still reads the signing fee (Q12): it does not change.

### v1.2.3-S6 · Out of Contract: the free Offer, and Under Offer · 2.5 sessions · opus

- *The Offer* (Q8). A Guild that wants a Hero Out of Contract offers a fee of 0. A Guild that answers
  through Offers, the player's, has `transferOfferDays` (3) to renew the Hero (S3), which withdraws
  it; otherwise, when it runs out, the Hero moves, signing the bidder's Contract, with the Transfer's
  Morale cost, Regard and lock. A Guild that answers at once, a Rival, answers by the rule below
  (DL-57's difference of controller). Two new ends for an Offer, renewed and taken
  (`TransferOfferEndCause`, `engine/src/events.ts`; `engine/src/transfer.ts`, `offerLapse`;
  `phases/offer-expiry.ts`).
- *Under Offer* (Q-G, Q-H). A Hero at home whom any Offer stands for, free or priced, is Under Offer:
  no Party can take them until it ends, declined, lapsed, withdrawn by a renewal or taken. A state of
  the Hero's read from the Offers, a selector and no stored field (data-model rule 1: availability is
  a selector). `dispatch` refuses them, `hero-unavailable` because `under-offer`, checked after
  `in-party` (`unavailability`, `engine/src/actions/dispatch.ts`, which today reads the Hero alone and
  now reads the Offers, so each caller passes them: `dispatch`, `availableHeroes` in `best-party.ts`,
  `preview.ts` and `web/src/state/availability.ts`); the Best Party's pool passes over them
  (`partyPool`), so neither the Rivals nor the policy ask. The refusal's words reach the CLI's
  `dispatch` and `preview` and the page (`words/src/refusals.ts`, `availability.ts`).
- *An Offer for a Hero away* (Q-H). `offerTransfer` no longer refuses `hero-in-party`. The Hero is
  Under Offer from their return, and the Offer's `expiresOnTurn` counts `transferOfferDays` from that
  night, their Party's `returnsOnTurn`, which `dispatch` sets and nothing moves, so the Offer stores
  it when made (`offerExpiresOnTurn`, `engine/src/transfer.ts`); `acceptTransfer` still waits for
  them home. `docs/data-model.md`'s offers and the validator's offer checks say so.
- *The answer* (Q15). A Rival, and the policy, holds a Hero Out of Contract and pays them until an
  Offer comes; then renews at its length if the Hero's Level is at or above its Roster's median, or
  its Roster is at its floor (`rivalRosterMin`; the policy's, a harness setting at 4), and lets them go
  otherwise (`engine/src/controllers/rival.ts`, `transferAnswer`; `harness/src/policy.ts`). The policy
  still declines every priced Offer (#20's rest).
- *Who bids* (the owner, Q-C). Every Guild's Heroes Out of Contract can be taken, the player's
  included. A Rival's own Transfer step and the Transfer Generator both weigh them on every Roster: a
  Rival's answered at once, the player's through a free Offer (`transferChoice`; `phases/transfers.ts`).
  The Generator still never takes a Hero under Contract from the player's Guild.
- *ADR-0002.* Its third exception is narrowed to Heroes under Contract, since a rule that binds every
  Guild's expired Heroes alike is the ADR's decision, not an exception to it. The ADR already holds
  three amendment notes, so it is restated as it now stands and its old wording archived to
  `docs/adr/archive/` (documentation.md, Writing a live page). With it, in this story:
  `engine/src/controller-reads.test.ts`'s entry for `phases/transfers.ts`; the **Transfer
  Generator**'s entry in `CONTEXT.md`; and the `stopped` line's wording in `harness/gate.json`, its
  limit unchanged.
- *The harness's assertion* (M6-S3, `harness/src/play.ts`): a Transfer naming the player's Guild is
  allowed when, and only when, it is a free Offer on a Hero Out of Contract that the policy let run
  out.

## 4. Risks

- *The free Offer reaches the player's Roster.* The harness's M6-S3 assertion and its policy were
  built on no Transfer ever naming the player's Guild. S6 changes the assertion. Its proof is a
  4-year run with refusals and stopped games at 0.
- *Under Offer keeps Heroes home* (Q-G). A Rival's priced Offer holds the player's Hero from Quests
  while it stands; the policy declines priced Offers at once, so in the harness only free Offers hold
  its Heroes. S8 counts the Days.
- *Ends in a quick run.* Founding Contracts end spread over their terms (Q-E), most within 2 years; a
  Contract signed in play ends 1 to 3 years on. S3, S6 and S7 run 4 years too.
- *Near, not equal.* Wages no longer follow Level, so a Guild whose Heroes grow pays less than today
  until it renews. The length's factor lowers wages by up to a tenth, and Out of Contract raises them
  by a tenth (Q-D). S2's run shows by how much.
- *The fee falls with time.* A Hero near expiry sells cheap, and the Rivals' debt sale raises less.
  S7's run reports the folds.
- *The fee unfitted* (Q-F, DL-312): at 0.3 a high-Level Hero costs up to about three times
  `v1.2.2`'s fee, and the Rivals' buying and debt sales move with it until `v1.2.4`; the close
  reports it.
- *Saves.* Refused once in the release (14), as the saves policy allows.
- *The bundle.* The Contract's controls add bytes; S9 checks the budget first.
- *The economy stays broken* until `v1.2.4` (Q20): the playtest meets Bankruptcy.
- *Size.* 15–19 sessions. S7's search and S8's harness lines are the most separable.
