# v1.2.3 — Contracts: a wage set at signing, a length chosen, and Heroes who leave when it ends

**Objective:** give every Hero in every Guild a Contract (DL-294 item 6). Its wage is set at signing
from the Hero's Level and Stats and fixed for its term, and the signer chooses its length, 1, 2 or 3
years, a longer term cheaper each period. A Guild may renew at any time, and may release a Hero with a
payout of most of the wages left. An expired Hero keeps playing and charging. Another Guild can take
them for no fee unless their Guild renews within 3 Days. The Transfer fee becomes the wages left on
the Contract. The Rivals and the harness's policy follow the same rules (ADR-0002). Every new figure
starts near `v1.2.2`'s cost (Q21), so the closing Gate shows what the rules do, not new magnitudes.

**Aims at:** sentence 5 of the [design](../../management-design.md), "If I don't renew her Contract,
someone else will take her." Every Contract has an end the player can see coming. At expiry, a free
Offer the player can answer only by renewing.

The owner's answers to Q6–Q12 and Q15 of `v1.2.2`'s epic (its §6), given on 2026-10-12, set this
release's rules; DL-294 item 6 records them. The open questions below (§6) are the agent's, put to
the owner before the story they block.

### v1.2.3-S2 · The Contract and its wage · 2.5 sessions · opus, rows sonnet

- *The record* (Q6). A Hero on a Roster holds `contract`: the wage, the Turn signed, the length in
  years and the end Day; a Hero on no Roster holds none. Its end is the Day the term's years run out,
  360 Days each (`engine/src/time.ts`). `schemaVersion` 14; `docs/data-model.md`. The validator checks
  a Roster Hero holds one and no other Hero does.
- *The wage* (Q6, Q7b), one rule reading the whole game, the Hero, their Guild and its Roster, so the
  backlog's terms join inside it (DL-294 item 8): (`wageBase` + `wagePerLevel` × Level + Stat sum ÷
  `wageStatDivisor` + `wageFameFactor` × the Guild's Fame) × the length's factor × (1 −
  Negotiation), rounded down once, whole Silver. `contractLengthsYears` [1, 2, 3] and
  `contractWageFactors` [1, 0.95, 0.9] are two config lists of one length, checked by `checkConfig`.
- *Charged.* `v1.2.2`'s wage charge reads each Contract's wage in place of `wagePerLevel` × Level; a
  Level gained no longer moves it. `guild.wages-paid` names each Hero's (`engine/src/selectors.ts`,
  `phases/guild-state.ts`).
- *Signed* at every signing: `recruit` and the Transfer actions take the length the signer chooses
  (`engine/src/actions/recruit.ts`, `actions/transfer.ts`). In a Transfer the buyer chooses and the
  old Contract ends. `hero.recruited` and `hero.transferred` carry the terms.
- *Who chooses* (Q7c). A Disposition's `contractYears`, Steady 2, Ambitious 1, Miserly 3, Reckless 1,
  by the kind's own steps (`content/dispositions.json`, `content/src/kinds/`); the policy 2 years, a
  harness setting (`harness/src/policy.ts`); every founding Contract, the player's and the Rivals',
  drawn evenly from the list on the `guilds` stream and signed on Day 0 (`engine/src/generators/guilds.ts`,
  `create.ts`). A new Rival's founding draws the same way.
- *Ended* by a Death, a Retirement, a Transfer, or a fold, whose Heroes join the pool with none
  (`phases/endings.ts`).

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] The wage rule's tests: each term, the factors, Negotiation, rounding; a Level gained leaving the
      wage as signed. The founding draw pinned on a seed.
- [ ] The validator's tests of the Contract's presence and its end.
- [ ] A quick run, `compare` with `v1.2.2`'s close: each side's wages a Guild-year near it.

### v1.2.3-S3 · Renewal, and the Contract's end · 1.5 sessions · opus, stop and words sonnet

- *Renewal* (Q9). A new action, `renew`: the Hero's Guild signs them again at any time, at the wage
  rule's price for their Level and Stats now and a length it chooses, with no fee. The new term starts
  that Day and the old one's rest is dropped. Its refusals: a Hero not on the Roster, away on a Quest
  (the owner, §6 Q-A), dead or retired. `hero.contract-renewed`.
- *The end.* On the night of its end Day the Contract expires: `hero.contract-expired`, a Milestone
  for the player's Guild. The Hero stays on the Roster, plays, and is charged their wage as before,
  until renewed, released or taken (S6). A stop, `contract-expired`, for the Guild Fast forward
  watches (`engine/src/stops.ts`). No stop before the end; the Roster and the Hero page show the Days
  left (S9).

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] `renew`'s tests: its price, the term replaced, each refusal; the expiry's night, the wage still
      charged, the stop.
- [ ] A 4-year quick run: Contracts expiring, and none renewed by the Rivals or the policy until S6.

### v1.2.3-S4 · Release, with a payout · 1 session · opus

- *Release* (Q10). A new action, `release`: during the term the Guild pays `releasePayoutFactor`
  (0.8) × the wages left, the wage × the wage charges left in the term; nothing once expired. The Hero
  joins the Unattached pool with no Contract, their gear returned to the Holding as a Transfer's is.
  Each teammate who held them warmly or better loses `transferMoraleCost`, as at a Transfer out
  (`engine/src/transfer.ts`). Refused for a Hero away on a Quest. `hero.released`, its payout in the
  payload.
- *The last Hero* (the owner, Q-B). A Guild may release its last Hero, and the game warns first. One
  selector, `releaseWarning`, names the case: the last Hero, and whether the Wealth left after the
  payout covers the cheapest Hero in the pool (`cheapestUnattached`). The page asks for a
  confirmation with that warning, and the CLI's `release` asks for `--confirm`. The engine refuses
  nothing for it: a Guild that cannot sign another Hero meets the "Roster wiped" ending, and for the
  player's Guild the game ends (`phases/endings.ts`). A Rival's or the policy's release follows the
  same rule, with no warning.
- *The ending* "Roster wiped" still reads the signing fee (Q12): it does not change.

**Acceptance:**

### v1.2.3-S5 · The Transfer fee from the wages left · 1 session · opus

- *The signing fee stays* (Q12), from the list price as today; `heroFee`'s shared core keeps only that use.
- `transferFeeFactor` takes its new meaning and default (§2).

### v1.2.3-S6 · Out of Contract: the free Offer · 2 sessions · opus

- *The Offer* (Q8). A Guild that wants an expired Hero offers a fee of 0. A Guild that answers
  through Offers, the player's, has `transferOfferDays` (3) to renew the Hero (S3), which withdraws
  it; otherwise, when it runs out, the Hero moves, signing the bidder's Contract, with the Transfer's
  Morale cost, Regard and lock. A Guild that answers at once, a Rival, answers by the rule below
  (DL-57's difference of controller). Two new ends for an Offer: renewed, and taken
  (`engine/src/transfer.ts`, `offerLapse`; `phases/offer-expiry.ts`).
- *The answer* (Q15). A Rival, and the policy, holds an expired Hero and pays them until an Offer
  comes; then renews at its length if the Hero's Level is at or above its Roster's median, or its
  Roster is at its floor (`rivalRosterMin`; the policy's, a harness setting at 4), and lets them go
  otherwise (`engine/src/controllers/rival.ts`, `transferAnswer`; `harness/src/policy.ts`). The policy
  still declines every priced Offer (#20's rest).
- *Who bids* (the owner, Q-C). Every Guild's expired Heroes can be taken, the player's included. A
  Rival's own Transfer step and the Transfer Generator both weigh them on every Roster: a Rival's
  answered at once, the player's through a free Offer (`transferChoice`; `phases/transfers.ts`).
  The Generator still never takes a Hero under Contract from the player's Guild. ADR-0002's third
  exception gets a dated amendment narrowing it to Heroes under Contract, since a rule that binds
  every Guild's expired Heroes alike is the ADR's decision, not an exception to it.
- *The harness's assertion* (M6-S3, `harness/src/play.ts`): a Transfer naming the player's Guild is
  allowed when, and only when, it is a free Offer on an expired Hero that the policy let run out.

## 4. Risks

- *The free Offer reaches the player's Roster.* The harness's M6-S3 assertion and its policy were
  built on no Transfer ever naming the player's Guild. S6 changes the assertion. Its proof is a
  4-year run with refusals and stopped games at 0.
- *Few ends in a quick run.* Founding Contracts end in years 1 to 3, so a 2-year run sees a third of
  them. S3, S6 and S7 run 4 years.
- *Near, not equal.* Wages no longer follow Level, so a Guild whose Heroes grow pays less than
  today until it renews. The length's factor lowers wages by up to a tenth. S2's run shows by how much.
- *The fee falls with time.* A Hero near expiry sells cheap, and the Rivals' debt sale raises less.
  S7's run reports the folds.
- *Saves.* Refused once in the release (14), as the saves policy allows.
- *The bundle.* The Contract's controls add bytes; S9 checks the budget first.
- *The economy stays broken* until `v1.2.4` (Q20): the playtest meets Bankruptcy.
- *Size.* 13–17 sessions. S7's search and S8's harness lines are the most separable.
