# v1.1.4 — Richer Quests: Checks as queries over the Party, and every Stat put to work

**Objective:** the owner's answers of 2026-10-02 (DL-148), as one v1.1.x patch. An analysis of the
Quest Engine found that the number of Heroes sent decides most Checks, not their Stats; that a Check's
weighted sum lets any Stat stand in for any other; that seven of the twenty Hero Stats are read by one
Encounter each; and that Morale, Hero Traits and the Check's margin change nothing on a Quest. This
release answers each:
- *Checks as queries.* A Check's score is a query over the Party's Heroes, written in content: the
  strongest Warrior's Strength, the total Intelligence of the Mages and Healers, the Wits of the least
  agile Hero. Synergies' requirements are written in the same language.
- *Several Checks to an Encounter.* Up to four named Checks, combined with `and`, `or` and
  `at-least`, each rolled.
- *Degrees.* A Check comes out a Triumph, a Success, a Setback or a Failure, and an Encounter's
  consequences can differ by Degree. A strong success needs Triumphs.
- *A Hero's form.* Each Hero's Morale band, and a wound taken on the Quest, scale what they add.
- *Harm that reads the Stats.* Who is hurt, and who shrugs it off, follow content's queries and
  Stats.
- *The chances shown.* The Board and the Party builder show what each Check reads and a word for the
  chance, with the numbers in the detail; the Rivals choose their Parties by the same estimate.
- *Traits that matter.* Hero Traits have effects, and Heroes rarely earn new ones on Quests.
- *Content widened.* About 24 Encounters, a "lore" kind, finales, gear for the Mental and Social
  Stats, and every Hero Stat read by at least three Checks.

**Sessions:** 15–19 · **Depends on:** `v1.1.3` released · **Implements:** DL-148.

**Gate:**
- *The decisions.* Each of DL-148's items is met, and a test drives each through the page's own
  controls or the CLI's.
- *Parity first.* After S1, with every Encounter and Synergy still in its old form, a core-only
  50 × 20 sweep's play counters equal `v1.1.3`'s for the same seeds, and creation hashes for 1,000
  seeds are unchanged.
- *The Quest target* (DL-83 item 4, DL-148 item 3). In the closing 50 × 20 sweep, the player's
  Quests fail 20 to 30% of the time and are strong successes at most 50%. The other DL-83 targets are
  measured and recorded, not tuned.
- *Coverage.* Every Hero Stat is read by at least three Checks across at least two Encounter kinds,
  held by a content test.
- *The estimate.* For sampled Parties and Quests, the chance estimate is within 2 percentage points
  of the resolver's rate over 20,000 seeds.
- *Rare Traits.* The closing sweep reports the Traits earned per Hero career; the mean is about one
  or fewer.
- *The script* is under 800 kB.
- *The page opens from `file://`*, and a Quest can be sent and returned there.
- *The views.* The Board, the Party builder, the Quest report, a Hero's page and the History open at
  360px, 820px and 1280px with no sideways scroll of the page, in the light and the dark scheme, and
  the guard test for capitals passes over every new word.
- *The release.* `v1.1-release` is fast-forwarded to `v1.1.4-dev` and pushed, the pull request CI
  raises is merged, and `v1.1.4` is deployed from CI with `e2e:live` passing on both addresses

### v1.1.4-S1 · The language: queries over the Party, and Synergies written in it · 2 sessions · opus

- *The glossary first* (principle 13). `CONTEXT.md` gains **Query**, **Degree** (with **Triumph**
  and **Setback**), **Form**, **Finale** and **Earned Trait**. **Check** says an Encounter has one to
  four, each named, and **Synergy** that its requirements are a Query.
- *ADR-0010.* Checks and Synergy requirements are Queries over the Party, written in content and
  evaluated by the engine. It extends ADR-0006: a new way to read the Party is a content change unless
  it needs a new operator.
- *The types* (`engine/src/content-types.ts`). A Query is a JSON array, operator first, of one of five
  types (DL-148 item 5):

  | Type | Forms |
  | --- | --- |
  | Set | `"party"`, `["where", Set, Pred]`, `["top", Set, n, Val]`, `["bottom", Set, n, Val]` |
  | Hero | `["best", Set, Val]`, `["worst", Set, Val]` |
  | Val, of the current Hero | `["stat", k]`, `["skill", k]`, `["level"]`, `["morale"]`, `["age"]`, `["weighted", [[k, w], …]]`, a number, `+`, `-`, `*` |
  | Pred, of the current Hero | `["class", k, …]`, `["background", k, …]`, `["sex", s]`, `["has-skill", k, min?]`, `["has-trait", k]`, `["hurt"]`, `>=`, `>`, `<`, `<=`, `=`, `and`, `or`, `not` |
  | Num, of the Party | `["sum" \| "avg" \| "max" \| "min", Set, Val]`, `["count", Set]`, `["ranked", Set, Val, [w1, w2, …]]`, `["of", Hero, Val]`, a number, `+`, `-`, `*`, `least`, `greatest` |

- *The evaluator* (`engine/src/quest-engine/query.ts`). It is pure and draws nothing.
  - `party` is the members still in the sequence, in Party order.
  - Effective Stats come from `effectiveStats`, read once per Hero per Encounter.
  - Ties in `best`, `worst`, `top` and `bottom` go to the earlier Hero in Party order, and sums run in
    Party order.
  - An empty Set gives 0 to every Num.
  - A Stat, Skill, Class or Trait that content no longer defines reads 0 or matches no one
    (data-model rule 5).
  - It reads only the Party's Heroes and their state on the Quest. The source-reading test that holds
    `check.ts` away from the Guild's leader and dealings (M4's third risk) covers `query.ts`.
- *The checks* (`content/src/validate.ts`). Each is refused with its path, in `refusals.test.ts`:
  - a node of the wrong type, or with the wrong number of arguments;
  - a key content does not define;
  - a Query of more than 64 nodes or deeper than 12;
  - `top` or `bottom` with `n` below 1 or not whole, and `ranked` weights negative or not finite;
  - `["sex", …]` anywhere but in a `where` filter, and a Set filtered by Sex counted by `count`.
    Sex decides who takes part, never a value (DL-148 item 6).
- *Synergies.* `requires` becomes a list of Party-level conditions, each a Num compared with a
  number (`[">=", Num, n]`), all of which must hold. The old `classes` and `skills` form loads into it:
  `[">=", ["count", ["where", "party", ["class", k]]], n]` and
  `[">=", ["max", "party", ["skill", k]], 1]`. `synergiesFor` and the preview read it unchanged in
  shape.
- *The old Encounter form loads.* `demands`, `aggregate`, `skillKeys` and `difficultyFactor` make one
  Check whose score is `["+", ["*", w1, [aggregate, "party", ["stat", s1]]], …]`, added up in the
  old order, so every result is the same to the last bit.
- *The content version* goes from 20 to 21 (the shapes). Nothing is re-authored.

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] Each of the owner's examples evaluates as its SQL reading says, among 40 or more evaluator tests:
      the strongest Warrior's Strength; the total Intelligence of the Mages and Healers; the Wits of
      the least agile Hero.
- [ ] Every refusal above names its path in `refusals.test.ts`.
- [ ] The core-only 50 × 20 sweep's play counters equal `v1.1.3`'s, and creation hashes for 1,000

### v1.1.4-S2 · Several Checks, Degrees and the pass formula · 2 sessions · opus

- *The Encounter's shape.*
  - `checks` is a list of one to four Checks: `key`, `name`, `score` (a Num Query), `skills`,
    `difficultyFactor`, and optionally `flat` and `variance`.
    - The target is `flat + difficultyFactor × difficulty`. With `flat` alone it ignores difficulty,
      for a count such as "at least one Hero with Strength 12".
    - `variance` replaces `checkVariance` for that Check; 0 makes a deterministic gate.
  - `pass` combines them: a Check's key, `["and", …]`, `["or", …]` or `["at-least", k, …]`. Every Check
    is named in it once. With one Check it may be left out.
  - `consequences` gains optional `triumph` and `setback` lists. A missing `triumph` uses `success`,
    and a missing `setback` uses `failure`, so an old Encounter runs as before.
- *The Check* (`check.ts`). Each Check's capability is its score, plus `skillBonusPerRating` × the
  best rating held of each of its `skills`, plus the Synergy term, times teamwork (and, from S3, times
  form), plus a consumable's bonus.
- *Degrees.* A Check's Degree comes from its roll ÷ target against `checkDegrees` (`triumph` 1.25,
  `success` 1, `setback` 0.75; below `setback` is a Failure). An Encounter's Degree follows its
  `pass`: `and` takes the worst, `or` the best, and `at-least k` the k-th best.
- *The draws* (`resolve.ts`). There is one `next` from `quests` for each Check, in the order listed,
  whether or not the formula is already decided. A reroll consumable rolls again each Check of a
  failed Encounter that fell short of Success, one draw each, and the new rolls stand.
  - A consumable's bonus adds to every Check of its Encounter, one Item an Encounter (DL-148
    item 13).
- *The band.* A success still needs more than half the Encounters at Success or better. A strong
  success needs every Encounter at Success or better and at least `strongTriumphShare` (0.5) of them
  Triumphs. This is DL-83 item 17's margin, made a share of Triumphs.
- *Events.* `quest.encounter` gains `degree`, `decidedBy` (the Check that set the Degree) and
  `checks`, each with its key, capability, roll, target, margin and Degree. `roll`, `target`, `margin`
  and `outcome` stay, with the deciding Check's values. An entry written before `v1.1.4` reads as its
  `outcome`.
- *The words.* The CLI's and the page's narration and the Quest report give each Encounter's Degree
  and the deciding Check's name: "They held the line, but their Nerve broke."
- *Configuration* goes from 12 to 13: `checkDegrees` and `strongTriumphShare`. Later stories add their
  fields under 13 before the release.

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] An Encounter of `(a and b) or c` takes the Degree its formula says for each of the 64
      combinations of three Checks' Degrees.
- [ ] A Quest draws one `next` for each Check, however the formula falls, and the Loot's draws follow
      as before.
- [ ] A band test for each rule: a majority at Success; strong with half Triumphs; not strong with
      fewer.
- [ ] The Quest report and the History read a Degree, and an entry written before `v1.1.4` reads as
      Success or Failure.
- [ ] `npm run lint`, `typecheck`, `test` and `e2e -w web` pass.

### v1.1.4-S3 · A Hero's form: Morale and wounds in the Check · 1 session · opus

- *Form.* Each Hero's form is their Morale band's `moraleCheckFactor` (`wretched` 0.85, `low` 0.93,
  `steady` 1, `high` 1.04, `elated` 1.08), times `hurtCheckFactor` (0.85) once they are hurt on this
  Quest (DL-148 items 9 and 10).
- *What it scales.* Only the Stat values a Hero adds into a score. Filters, the choice of the best or
  worst Hero, Synergy requirements, Skill ratings, Level, age and counts read the Hero unscaled
  (item 11). So a hurt Warrior is still "the strongest Warrior", and contributes less.
- *Working Morale.* A Hero's Morale on the Quest is their Morale at departure plus the Morale
  consequences applied so far, held to the scale. It sets their band for form and is what
  `["morale"]` reads. Their stored Morale still changes on the return, as now.
- *The words.* The Party builder gives one Morale line for the Party, worded from its mean form:
  "Raises the odds", "Makes no difference", "Lowers the odds a little" or "Lowers the odds badly".
  No Hero's Morale is named (item 12).
- *The narration.* When a Check's Degree would have been another with every form at 1, from the same
  roll, the Encounter's narration says so once, for the Party: "Low spirits told against them" or
  "Their high spirits carried them through". `quest.encounter`'s Check gains `formDecided`.
- *Configuration:* `moraleCheckFactor` and `hurtCheckFactor`.

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] A test for each scaled and each unscaled read, through `query.ts`.
- [ ] A Morale consequence in Encounter 1 changes form in Encounter 2.
- [ ] The narration's sentence appears exactly when `formDecided` is set, and names no Hero.
- [ ] `npm run lint`, `typecheck` and `test` pass.

### v1.1.4-S4 · Harm that reads the Stats · 1–1.5 sessions · opus

- *Injury and Death* gain optional `among` (a Set Query), `exposedBy` and `guardedBy` (lists of Stat
  keys). *Morale* gains `steadiedBy` (DL-148 item 14).
  - `among` is narrowed to the Heroes not hurt on this Quest. If that leaves no one, it is the whole
    unhurt Party, so that leaving the Warriors at home never dodges the front rank's Injuries.
  - `exposedBy` weights the draw of who is hit: 1 + `exposureRange` (2) × (1 − the Hero's mean share
    of those Stats' content range).
  - `guardedBy` scales the chance by 1 − `guardRange` (0.5) × the chosen Hero's mean share.
  - `steadiedBy` scales each Hero's Morale losses by the same rule. Gains are not scaled.
  - Shares read effective Stats, so armour's Constitution protects.
- *The draws stay three an Injury and two a Death.* For an Injury: `u`, then who (`weightedPick`),
  then the Days; it lands if `u` < chance × guard. With none of the fields, the weights are all 1
  and the guard 1, which draws exactly what `pick` and `chance` drew.
- *The outcome.* `QuestOutcome` gains `moraleByHero`, and `quest.resolved` carries it. `moraleDelta`
  stays. `questsConclude` applies each Hero's own.
- *Configuration:* `exposureRange` and `guardRange`.

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] With none of the new fields, a 50 × 20 sweep's play counters equal S3's.
- [ ] `among` falls back to the whole unhurt Party when it matches no one.
- [ ] Over 20,000 draws, a Hero at the bottom of `exposedBy`'s Stat is hit about three times as often
      as one at the top, and `guardedBy` at the top halves the chance.
- [ ] `npm run lint`, `typecheck` and `test` pass.

### v1.1.4-S5 · The chance estimate · 1.5 sessions · opus

- *A selector* (`engine/src/quest-engine/estimate.ts`). It draws nothing. It reads the proposed
  Party, its consumables, form, teamwork and Synergies.
  - *One Check.* The roll is C × (1 + v × (2u − 1)), so P(roll ≥ T) = 1 − clamp((T ÷ C − 1 + v) ÷ 2v,
    0, 1), and a step at v = 0. Each Degree's chance follows from its threshold.
  - *An Encounter.* Every combination of its Checks' Degrees is counted (at most 4⁴ = 256), with a
    reroll folded in.
  - *The Quest.* The band's chances follow from each Encounter's chance of Success and of Triumph.
  - It assumes no one falls, and says so.
- *Chance bands* (`chanceBands` in configuration). `hopeless` below 0.10, `unlikely` to 0.35, `even`
  to 0.65, `likely` to 0.90, and `near-certain` from 0.90 (DL-148 item 15). The engine returns the
  key, and the front ends word it.
- *What a Check reads* (`describeQuery`). The engine returns a typed account of a Query: what it
  aggregates, its filters, the Stats it reads with their weights where it is a weighting, and its
  Skills. It returns no prose (ADR-0003); the CLI and the page word it: "Strength of the strongest
  Warrior".

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] For 200 sampled Parties and Quests, each Encounter's and the Quest's estimate is within 2
      percentage points of the resolver's rate over 20,000 seeds, with no one falling.
- [ ] `describeQuery` accounts for every operator, and both front ends word each.
- [ ] `npm run lint`, `typecheck` and `test` pass.

### v1.1.4-S7 · Hero Traits with effects, and Traits earned · 2 sessions · opus

- *A Hero Trait's effects* (the Trait effect union). Either or both of:
  - `statModifiers`, applied through `effectiveStats` as gear is, so they show wherever a Stat shows;
  - `encounterBonus`: a `flat` added to every Check of an Encounter of its `kinds` or `tags`, once for
    the Party however many members hold the Trait (DL-148 item 26).
- *The four Injury Traits* (item 24). Lame is Dexterity −2, Endurance −1. One-eyed is Perception −2,
  Weaponry −1. Short-winded is Endurance −2, Constitution −1. Scarred is Courage +1, Charisma −1.
- *The `trait` consequence:* `{ "kind": "trait", "traitKey", "chance", "among" }` (item 25).
  - A positive Trait, on a Triumph, is drawn when the consequence applies, held, and granted only if
    the Quest ends a strong success.
  - A negative Trait, on a Failure, falls only among the Heroes that same Encounter injured.
  - A Hero gains at most one Trait a Quest. A Trait already held is never granted, and an excluded one
    is not either (`excludes`, as Guildmaster Traits have).
  - Its draws are taken whether or not it lands: two, from `quests`, in the sequence, before Loot's.
- *The event.* `hero.trait-gained`, naming the Hero, the Trait, the Quest and the Encounter, a
  Milestone (item 27). The History, the Quest report and a Hero's page show it, and a Hero's page
  shows each Trait's effects.
- *R24* is as amended by DL-148.
- *Content:* the four Injury Traits' effects. The earned Traits are written in S10, after the owner's
  review; S7's tests use their own.

**Acceptance:**

- [ ] A Trait's Stat change shows on a Hero's page and in a Check, and its bonus applies once however

- [ ] A positive Trait drawn on a Triumph is granted on a strong success and not on a plain one.
- [ ] A negative Trait falls only on a Hero that Encounter injured.

## 4. Risks

- *The script's limit.* The script is 770 of 800 kB. The evaluator, the estimate and the words should
  take 6 to 10 kB, and the content's JSON more. `check-bundle` is read at each story's close.
- *Writing the content is the long pole.* The aggregates differ in scale, so a Check's factor means
  nothing without calibration. S9's report comes before any writing, and the review page shows the
  rates beside every Check.
- *The estimate differs from play,* since a Party can lose a Hero or Morale on the way. It says "if
  no one falls", and S5's test holds it to the resolver where no one does.
- *The Rivals change the baseline.* The harness's automatic policy uses `partyFor`, so the M8
  baseline DL-83 measures against moves in S6. S10's DL entry records the policy's change beside the
  numbers.
- *Rarer Loot.* Strong successes, which Loot needs, get rarer, and finales add a condition. The owner
  accepted this, with Loot to be tuned later (DL-148 item 23).
- *Sex in queries.* Validation holds it to filters and refuses counting a Set filtered by it (S1), so
  no Check scores by Sex.
- *A changed draw.* S1's parity sweep and S4's "none of the fields" sweep hold the draws, and each
  later change of play is explained in its DL entry.
- *Earned Traits stack up.* At most one a Quest, each only once, chances of 5 to 10%, positive ones
  only on strong successes, and the career mean reported.
