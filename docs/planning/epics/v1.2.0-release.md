# v1.2.0 — Fewer, weightier Quests: tiered Quest templates, Beats, Death by tier and the tools to write them

**Objective:** make each Quest a choice that costs something. A Quest template offers Tiers, each
longer, richer and with more Beats than the one below, so a Quest's weight is visible and the Board
grows harder with the world. Death falls on the hardest Tiers and the Board marks them Deadly. Every
Party is two Heroes or more. The tiers, the Death factor and the Board's size are settings, so the
line's later releases tune them without code. Content is built to grow: one file per row, editor
commands and drafting skills.

**Aims at** (the [Guild Management Design](../../management-design.md)'s sentences, Q20 c):
- "He needs another year of development." A Tier is a visible bar, and the Deadly Tiers punish a Hero
  sent too early.
- "I need to give him a chance." The Party floor of two and the XP divided by the Party make bringing
  a junior along a real cost.

The owner's answers to Q1–Q26 (§6) set the scope; DL-257 records them.

## 4. Risks

- *Size.* 19–24 sessions. S9–S11 are the most separable; if the release must be cut, S5's converted
  content plays, and the tools and the written content can follow as v1.2.1.
- *Income without tuning.* Fewer, longer Quests, Q23's neutral factors aside, may bankrupt the
  harness's player or the Rivals. It is reported (Q11 a). If the playtest cannot reach its second
  year, the owner decides then.
- *Working lines in play.* S5's converted Beats carry provisional lines until S11. No release ships
  between them.
- *A Best Party with no maximum.* Larger searches may slow a Turn. S8's profile and the copy-share line
  watch it.
- *The browser's content import.* S3's build-time import must keep the bundle within its check and the
  content identical; the content line of the test suite holds it.
- *Encounters drawn short.* A Tier with more Beats than its pools can fill is refused by the content
  check and `quests doctor`.

## 5. What the plan rests on

- The Board refills to `boardSize` 6 each morning; durations are drawn apart from difficulty, 2–10 Days
  by template; reward is difficulty × 4 to 5, duration never counted (`engine/src/generators/quests.ts`,
  `content/quest-templates.json`).

  most one Party an afternoon (`engine/src/actions/dispatch.ts`, `engine/src/controllers/rival.ts`).

  first by Fame 91.6%, 0 Bankruptcies (`harness/reports/v1.1.9-gate-2026-10-10.md`).
