# v1.1.2 — Portraits, Guild Banners and Unique Items' pictures, the Guildmaster described, and every standalone text beginning with a capital

**Objective:** the owner's six requests of 2026-09-30 (DL-125), as one v1.1.x patch:
- a `media/` folder the page reads;
- a 3 × 4 Portrait for the Guildmaster, Heroes and Patrons, with Silhouettes by sex, and the
  player's Guildmaster's Portrait chosen or drawn at setup from the owner's pictures;
- the Guildmaster's Birthplace, Appearance and Biography written from content, on request and
  for every blank;
- a Guild Banner beside each Guild's title;
- a Unique Item's picture beside its name;
- every standalone text beginning with a capital, by a written rule and a guard test.

**Sessions:** 7–9 · **Depends on:** `v1.1.1` (`main` at `a1b4178`) · **Implements:** DL-125 to
DL-127.

**Gate:**
- Each of DL-125's items 1 to 9 is met, and a test drives each through the page's own controls, or
  the CLI's for the engine's side.
- *The page opens from `file://`.* A browser spec opens the built page over `file://` and sees a
  picture and a Silhouette load. The bundle gate passes, and the deploy uploads the pictures beside
  the one script.
- *Play is unchanged.* A 50 × 20 sweep's counters equal `v1.1.1`'s for the same seeds, since no
  existing draw moves (DL-125, Consequences).
- *The words.* The guard test passes over every view, and `docs/agents/ui-text.md` holds the rule.
- *The views.* Every view opens at 360px, 820px and 1280px with no sideways scroll of the page, in
  the light and the dark scheme, and its screenshots are recorded.
- *The release.* `v1.1-release` is fast-forwarded to `v1.1.2-dev` and pushed, the pull request CI
  raises is merged, and `v1.1.2` is deployed from CI with `e2e:live` passing on both addresses
  (DL-124).

**Status:** built and verified — 2026-09-30 (DL-125 to DL-133). S7, the stream's rename, was added
on 2026-10-01 (DL-135) and built and verified the same day (DL-137). Released as `v1.1.2` on 2026-10-01 (pull request
#45).

## 4. Risks

- *`file://` in other browsers.* Only Chromium was measured, and Firefox and Safari restrict
  `file://` differently. A picture that fails shows its Silhouette, so the page never breaks. The
  `file://` spec runs in Chromium, as the other specs do.
- *The words change many tests.* S1 comes first, so later stories are written against the final
  words.
- *The script's size.* It is 649 kB, and the Silhouettes must stay in it small. S2 records the
  size.
- *A picture renamed after release* breaks the saves that hold its key. Names are never changed
  (DL-125 item 11), and the unit test cannot see a rename: review watches for it.
- *Schema 5 in a patch.* A `v1.1.1` page cannot load a `v1.1.2` save, and a `v1.1.2` page refuses
  a `v1.1.1` save. Players are told in words (S3), and a rollback reaches only to `v1.1.2`
  (DL-124 item 9).
- *A narrative may not suit every Guildmaster of its picture.* It names no age and no name, and the
  player may edit it at setup (DL-127 item 1).
