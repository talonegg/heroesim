# v1.1.2 — Portraits, Guild Banners and Unique Items' pictures, the Guildmaster described, and every standalone text beginning with a capital

**Objective:** the owner's six requests of 2026-09-30 (DL-125), as one v1.1.x patch:
- a `media/` folder the page reads;
- a 3 × 4 Portrait for the Guildmaster, Heroes and Patrons, with Silhouettes by sex, and the
  player's Guildmaster's Portrait chosen or drawn at setup from the owner's pictures;
- the Guildmaster's Birthplace, Appearance and Biography written from content, on request and
  for every blank;
- a Guild Banner beside each Guild's title;
- a Unique Item's picture beside its name;
- every standalone text beginning with a capital, by a written rule and a guard test.

**Sessions:** 7–9 · **Depends on:** `v1.1.1` (`main` at `a1b4178`) · **Implements:** DL-125.

**Gate:**
- Each of DL-125's items 1 to 9 is met, and a test drives each through the page's own controls, or
  the CLI's for the engine's side.
- *The page opens from `file://`.* A browser spec opens the built page over `file://` and sees a
  picture and a Silhouette load. The bundle gate passes, and the deploy uploads the pictures beside
  the one script.
- *Play is unchanged.* A 50 × 20 sweep's counters equal `v1.1.1`'s for the same seeds, since no
  existing draw moves (DL-125, Consequences).
- *The words.* The guard test passes over every view, and `docs/agents/ui-text.md` holds the rule.
- *The views.* Every view opens at 360px, 820px and 1280px with no sideways scroll of the page, in
  the light and the dark scheme, and its screenshots are recorded.
- *The release.* `v1.1-release` is fast-forwarded to `v1.1.2-dev` and pushed, the pull request CI
  raises is merged, and `v1.1.2` is deployed from CI with `e2e:live` passing on both addresses
  (DL-124).

**Status:** planned — 2026-09-30 (DL-125). Not started: the build waits for the owner's word.

### v1.1.2-S1 · Every standalone text begins with a capital · 1–1.5 sessions · opus

- *The rule* (DL-125 item 9).
  - `docs/agents/ui-text.md` is written with DL-125, and `AGENTS.md` points to it.
  - This story makes the page keep it.
- *The words.* Lower-case data is capitalised by `capitalised()` (`web/src/state/names.ts`) in the
  selector that builds each text, never by CSS. These are the audit's findings of 2026-09-30:
  - Whereabouts chips on the Roster and a Hero's page ("At the Hall", "Away with a Party, back on
    …", "Injured, healing on …", "In the Unattached pool", "Died on Day N"). They then read as the
    Board's already do, from one place (`web/src/state/selectors.ts`).
  - "None" wherever a part is empty (`hero-page.tsx`, `guildmaster.tsx`, `patrons.tsx`, `ui.tsx`'s
    default, and a Hero's empty Gear slot).
  - The Kind cells ("Weapon Gear", "Consumable", "…, Unique Item", from `kindOf` in
    `web/src/state/trade-views.ts`), matching the Kind filter's chips.
  - Regard band chips, the Outlook chip, the pool's Signs ("Yes", "Needs Fame 12"), Encounter chips
    ("Combat ×2"), and "Cannot be priced".
  - The Disposition on a Guild page's line: "Rival (Steady)", as the Guilds table has it
    (`web/src/state/names.ts`).
  - The History's choice "The World History", as its page title has it (`web/src/state/history.ts`).
- *The terms.*
  - "Stop the Fast forward" and "More ways to Fast forward" (`status-bar.tsx`).
  - "Accept the Offer" and "Answer the Offer" (`offers.tsx`, `stop.tsx`).
  - "{Name}'s Gear".
  - "… Consumable used" (`history.ts`).
  - The new-game disclosure's hint: "Sex, Age, Traits …".
  - The help's lower-case terms (`section.tsx`).
  - "Unattached pool" and "Debug: Whole History" stay as they are.
- *The guard.* A test renders each view and the Guild's four parts over a seeded game, from the
  page's test support, and fails any standalone text that begins with a lower-case letter. It
  checks headings, buttons, table headers and cells, chips, legends, labels and `dt`s, and allows
  numbers and names. It lists every failure, not the first.
- *The tests.* About 110 lines move with the words, mostly the units' exact names. The browser
  specs' `exact: true` names and `toHaveText`s are among them.

**Acceptance:**

## 4. Risks

- *`file://` in other browsers.* Only Chromium was measured, and Firefox and Safari restrict
  `file://` differently. A picture that fails shows its Silhouette, so the page never breaks. The
  `file://` spec runs in Chromium, as the other specs do.
- *The words change many tests.* S1 comes first, so later stories are written against the final
  words.
- *The script's size.* It is 649 kB, and the Silhouettes must stay in it small. S2 records the
  size.
- *A picture renamed after release* breaks the saves that hold its key. Names are never changed
  (DL-125 item 11), and the unit test cannot see a rename: review watches for it.
- *Schema 5 in a patch.* A `v1.1.1` page cannot load a `v1.1.2` save, and a `v1.1.2` page refuses
  a `v1.1.1` save. Players are told in words (S3), and a rollback reaches only to `v1.1.2`
  (DL-124 item 9).
- *A written Appearance may contradict the face chosen,* until the owner decides whether a
  Portrait's row carries appearance words (§0).
