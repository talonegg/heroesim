# v1.1.3 — Portraits that follow the person, Heroes described, and a portrait tool

**Objective:** the owner's requests of 2026-10-01 (DL-134, DL-135), as one v1.1.x patch:
- *Pictures that fit.* A Portrait for every Guildmaster and Hero, chosen to fit a person already
  drawn, from each picture's apparent age, station, temper, callings or Classes, features and hooks
  in content. The Silhouette shows where no picture fits.
- *Two streams.* Guildmasters' Portraits are drawn on `gm-portraits`, which v1.1.2-S7 names, and
  Heroes' on `hero-portraits`.
- *The Biography.* A Guildmaster's is written from their picture's station, callings, temper and
  hooks.
- *Heroes described.* Every Hero gains an Appearance and a Biography.
- *The page.* A picture grid at setup, the pictures on the detail pages, and thumbnails in the
  Roster, the pool and the Rivals list.
- *A portrait tool.* A command-line tool and Claude Code skills that:
  - convert uploads;
  - find duplicates;
  - write each new picture's rows, read by one agent and checked by a second;
  - analyse the whole corpus into a dated report and recommended JSON;
  - apply that JSON when the owner chooses.

**Sessions:** 11–14 · **Depends on:** `v1.1.2` released, with v1.1.2-S7's `gm-portraits` ·
**Implements:** DL-134 and DL-135.

**Gate:**
- *The decisions.* Each of DL-134's and DL-135's items that this epic builds is met, and a test
  drives each through the page's own controls, the CLI's for the engine's side, or the tool's
  commands.
- *Play is unchanged.* A core-only 50 × 20 sweep's play counters equal `v1.1.2`'s for the same
  seeds. No rule reads a Portrait or a text, and every new draw is on a new stream or on
  `gm-portraits`.
- *The tool.* The owner's pictures went in through `intake` (S4). Its report and the first
  `analyse` run's report and JSON are committed. `apply` put the reviewed JSON in place, and
  `npm run portraits -- check` passes.
- *The limits* (DL-134 item 9). Each is held by a check:
  - the script under 800 kB;
  - each picture under 120 kB;
  - each narrative within 260 characters, each hook within 160, and the longest Biography within
    1,000;
  - the setup grid reading about 250 kB of pictures or less.
- *Saves.* Schema 6 is written, and schema 5 is refused in DL-130's words.
- *The page opens from `file://`*, with a Rival's Portrait and a thumbnail loading.
- *The views.* Every view opens at 360px, 820px and 1280px with no sideways scroll of the page, in
  the light and the dark scheme, and its screenshots are recorded.
- *The release.* `v1.1-release` is fast-forwarded to `v1.1.3-dev` and pushed, the pull request CI
  raises is merged, and `v1.1.3` is deployed from CI with `e2e:live` passing on both addresses

### v1.1.3-S2 · The portrait tool: convert, register, fingerprint, check · 1.5–2 sessions · opus

- *The package.* `tools/portraits`, a workspace package in TypeScript, is run as
  `npm run portraits -- <command>`. Its tests run with the rest in `npm test`. It depends on
  `@playwright/test`, as `web` does, for Chromium, and joins no image package (DL-135 item 11).
- *Conversion.* This moves from `web/scripts/convert-picture.mjs`, which is removed, and its
  cropping to 3 × 4 is kept.
  - Each picture is written at 480 × 640, at most 120 kB, stepping the quality down from 0.8
    until it fits. An upload that cannot fit is refused with its size.
  - Each also gets a 160 × 213 thumbnail, `<key>.thumb.webp`, of about 10 kB.
- *The register.* `media/portraits/registry.json` holds every key ever issued, with its role, sex,
  perceptual hash and `live` or `retired`.
  - `nextKey(role, sex)` gives the next number, and never one issued before.
  - The existing pictures are registered, their hashes computed from their WebP.
  - The two originals in `docs/images/portraits/` are removed, since originals are not kept
    (DL-135 item 1). Their WebP stay.
- *The fingerprint.* A difference hash (dHash) of the picture drawn small in Chromium. `similar`
  lists the pictures within a distance, tuned on the owner's 40 (DL-136): the two originals of the
  pictures in the game must be found similar to their WebP, and the 38 others must not. The
  distance is written down in the register.
- *The inbox.* `media/inbox/guildmaster/` and `media/inbox/hero/` are ignored by git, with a
  `.gitkeep` in each.
  - Each may hold `male/` and `female/` subfolders, as the owner's source does (DL-136).
  - A picture's subfolder is the owner's word for its sex, and the checker flags a reading that
    differs.
- *The commands.*
  - `convert <source>` writes a picture and its thumbnail beside it, for a trial.
  - `fingerprint <file>` prints a hash and the nearest pictures.
  - `check` confirms:
    - every live register key has its picture and thumbnail in `media/`, and every picture its key;
    - every live key is listed in `content/portraits.json` and has a row in
      `portrait-appearances.json`;
    - every size is within its limit.
  - `write-rows <draft.json>` takes the agents' drafted rows and does the rest of intake as one
    transaction, undone as a whole on any failure:
    1. converts each picture;
    2. names it by `nextKey`;
    3. adds its rows;
    4. raises the content version;
    5. runs the content checks and `check`;
    6. deletes the uploads it took in.
  - `apply <run>` puts an analysis run's JSON in place, raises the content version and runs the
    checks, undone as a whole on failure.
- *The records* are written by the tool from the agents' drafts:
  - an intake report, `docs/portraits/intake/<date>.md`;
  - an analysis run, `docs/portraits/analysis/<date>.md` and the folder `<date>/`, with `-2` for a
    second run on a day.

### v1.1.3-S3 · The skills and agents: intake and analyse · 1.5–2 sessions · opus

- *The agents* (`.claude/agents/`), both run on Opus (DL-135 item 11).
  - `portrait-reader` looks at one picture and drafts its row. It follows `CONTEXT.md` and DL-127:
    - it describes no clothing in a narrative;
    - it names no ethnicity;
    - it keeps to the bounds;
    - it takes tags only from the vocabulary, or proposes a new term as such.
  - `portrait-checker` looks at the picture beside the draft, and confirms or disputes each field
    with its reason.
- *The skills* (`.claude/skills/`).
  - `portrait-intake`, for each upload in the inbox:
    1. `fingerprint`;
    2. for a near match, the reader compares the two and reports a duplicate, a variant or
       different;
    3. the reader drafts, and the checker checks;
    4. `write-rows` takes the agreed rows;
    5. the report lists each picture, what was read and why, every disagreement, and each
       duplicate and variant left in the inbox.
  - `portrait-analyse`:
    1. reads every live picture again against its row;
    2. counts the corpus by role, sex, band and Class;
    3. finds the gaps, text over its bounds, unused vocabulary, duplicates and variants, and the
       pictures whose clothing or setting may be anachronistic. Those are listed for the owner, and
       never held back (DL-137);
    4. drafts the sentences missing or short, for each station, calling and band, temper, and a
       Hero's Background, Class and Trait;
    5. writes the report and a recommended `portraits.json`, `portrait-appearances.json` and
       `descriptions.json`;
    6. orders the pictures to commission next.

    It changes nothing live.
  - `portrait-apply` runs `apply <run>` for a run the owner names, after showing its diff.
- *Guardrails.*
  - A skill never commits or pushes.
  - A variant is never added or swapped without the owner.
  - A sentence or row the agent wrote is marked the agent's in the report, for the owner to
    change.
- *`docs/agents/portraits.md`* says how to run each skill, and `AGENTS.md` points to it.

## 4. Risks

- *Thin coverage shows Silhouettes.* The Class and band must match, and Guildmasters never repeat.
  The analysis report counts the gaps, and S5's and S6's counters show how often a Silhouette
  shows.
- *A misread picture* gives a face to the wrong age or Class. The checker reads every field again,
  and the owner reviews every diff.
- *The fingerprint's distance.* Set too tight, it misses a redrawn picture; set too loose, it asks
  the agent too often. It is tuned on the owner's 40 in S2 and written in the register, and the agent
  decides each near match.
- *Keys are permanent.* A save holds its people's keys. A retired picture's key stays in the
  register, and a save naming it loads with a warning and shows the Silhouette, as a Guildmaster's
  unknown key does today.
- *Save size.* Hero texts add up to about 1.3 kB a Hero. S6 measures it against localStorage's
  quota, and the acceptance holds the largest at year 20 under 2.5 MB.
- *Schema 6 in a patch.* A `v1.1.3` page refuses a `v1.1.2` save. There are no v1.1.2 saves in
  players' hands (DL-135 item 7), and a rollback reaches only to `v1.1.3` (DL-124 item 9).
- *The agents' words.* Every narrative, hook and sentence is the agent's reading, marked as such in
  its report, for the owner to change.
