# v1.2.2 — Coin and the Hall: Wealth in Gold and Silver, and a Hall that costs to keep

**Objective:** lay the economy's ground before its pass (DL-294). Wealth is held in whole Silver and
shown as Gold and Silver. The Hall's Facilities become content that can be extended, each with an
upkeep that rises with its grade, and the Hall itself costs a base and a share for each Hero, every 30
Days. Wages stay as they are until `v1.2.3` and are charged apart from the Hall. One Upkeep becomes a
month of costs. A loot-only Item carries its own value, and every Guild sells what it does not need.
The Treasury reads income and expenses, a line for each. Every new figure starts near today's cost
(Q21), so the closing Gate shows what the rules do, not new magnitudes.

**Aims at:** none of the design's nine sentences; `v1.2.3`'s Contracts aim at sentence 5. The
playtest asks whether the Treasury tells the player where the Wealth comes from and goes (agent's
reading, for the owner to see).

The owner's answers to Q1–Q22 (§6), given on 2026-10-12 from their outline of the Guild's income and
expenses, set the scope of this release and the next two; DL-294 records them.

**Sessions:** 12–15 (agent's estimate) · **Depends on:** `v1.2.1` released (pull request #66, merged
as `72d14e9`), on `v1.2.2-dev` from `a93f208` · **Implements:** DL-294 items 1 to 5; the surplus part
of #25; the ground of #51's Wealth and Market tuning.

## 4. Risks

- *Facilities as content* is the largest story: four effects move from config and code to rows, and
  the Rivals' and the policy's builds stop naming a Facility. S3's proof is that play does not move.
- *Near, not equal.* A new Guild pays about 50 a month more, and a Thrifty Guildmaster's wages rise a
  tenth, the Trait leaving them (Q17). S4's run shows by how much.
- *Saves.* Refused twice in the release (12, 13), as the saves policy allows.
- *The bundle.* The coins element and the Treasury's sections add bytes; S8 checks the budget first.
- *The economy stays broken* until `v1.2.4` (Q20): the playtest meets Bankruptcy.
- *Size.* 12–15 sessions. S6's surplus rule and S7's harness lines are the most separable.

## 5. What the plan rests on

Facts checked on `a93f208`, 2026-10-12:
- Wealth is one number a Guild holds, checked as a number, every rule rounding its own result
  (`engine/src/document.ts`, `validate.ts`, `selectors.ts`, `market.ts`); no formatter shows it.
- Upkeep is `wagePerLevel` × Level over the living Roster plus `hallMaintenancePerGrade` × the Hall's
  grades, × the Guildmaster's `upkeepFactor`, one charge every 10 Days on Days 9, 19, 29, …
  (`engine/src/selectors.ts`, `phases/guild-state.ts`).
- The Facilities are a tuple in code; grades are content rows priced 200, 500 and 1,000, with no
  upkeep (`engine/src/document.ts`, `content/improvements.json`, `content/src/kinds/improvements.ts`).
- Items sell at `sellRatio` 0.5 of their `buyPrice`; five loot-only Items at `lootOnlyPrice` 600;
  Rivals and the policy never sell (`engine/src/market.ts`).
- The editor has kinds for config, Traits and Quests; none for Dispositions, Items or Improvements
  (`tools/editor/src/`).
- `v1.2.1`'s close: every game of the player's bankrupt; Upkeep 94.7% of the player's spending; rewards
  32,880.6 against Upkeep 31,347.8 a Guild-year (DL-292).
