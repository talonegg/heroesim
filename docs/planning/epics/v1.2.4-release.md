# v1.2.4 — The economy pass: Guilds that can live on the finished rules

**Objective:** tune the economy that `v1.2.2` and `v1.2.3` finished (DL-294 item 7), so that a Guild
can live under `v1.2.1`'s Fatigue and form. The player stays solvent in most games. The Rivals fail
fewer Quests, recruit, and fold within DL-83's band. Games reach 20 years, so that the save is
measured there again. Then Fatigue is tuned to its bands, the work `v1.2.1`-S11 stopped (DL-292). The
pass changes settings and content rows only, each change measured alone in a full Gate. A change to a
rule is put to the owner with its trial before it is built (§6). #51 closes with it.

**Aims at:** no new sentence; it restores the ground the design's sentences stand on. The owner's
playtest, deferred from `v1.2.1` (DL-293), is scored by that release's two sentences, "I can't send
her; she's exhausted" and "I need to give him a chance", and by `v1.2.3`'s sentence 5, "If I don't
renew her Contract, someone else will take her."

The owner deferred the pass (DL-293) and placed it after the redesign (DL-294, Q20). The bands it
tunes against are the owner's to approve first (§2), as DL-257 asked: "the targets reset and tuned
against, once more of the line is built".

**Sessions:** 10–14, plus the sweeps' wall clock, about 25 minutes a full `gate` (agent's estimate) ·
**Depends on:** `v1.2.3` released, its closing Gate the baseline · **Implements:** #51 items 1 and 4;
DL-292's economy release; DL-290 item 5's bands, once approved.

**Gate:**
- *Correct and fast.* At the close, a full `gate` alone meets every line of `harness/gate.json`,
  the save after 20 years included, now that games reach it. The estimate's two limits hold.
- *The bands* the owner approved at S1 are met at the close, or each one missed is explained and
  accepted on the owner's word. This release's Targets block, as earlier releases' did not (Q20).
- *Each change measured alone.* Every setting or row changed has its own full `gate` against the one
  before it, recorded with its report.
- *The boxes.* The engine's twenty-year boxes, the boxes kept apart and the speed run, each alone at
  the close.
- *The doctors.* `traits doctor`, `quests doctor` and `facilities doctor` before and after every
  story that changes content.
- *The owner's playtest* of a few in-game years, scored by the three sentences above.

**Status:** Planned 2026-10-12 (DL-294); not started. Its §2 bands wait for the owner at S1.

## 4. Risks

- *Memory.* A full `gate` holds about 1.3 GB a game process, 8 at once; a `v1.2.1` trial was stopped
  by the system for low memory (DL-292). Trials run one at a time, nothing else alongside.
- *Wall clock.* A full `gate` that plays 20 years took about 25 minutes at `v1.2.0`'s close; a
  story of five trials is about two hours of runs.
- *Levers that pull against each other.* The player's rewards are the Rivals' lost Quests, and the
  Rivals' Wealth sets the Transfer market. S2 and S3 each re-read the other's Targets.
- *A band that no setting reaches* is reported with its trials, and the owner chooses: a rule change,
  a new band, or the miss accepted (§6).
- *The save.* Games that reach 20 years bring the save back to about 17 MiB gzipped. The Gate's mean
  under 30 MiB holds unless the Rosters grow again (R1).
