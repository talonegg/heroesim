# v1.2.4 — The economy rebased: Plunder, a new starting point, and Guilds that can live on it

**Objective:** rebase the economy that `v1.2.2` and `v1.2.3` finished (DL-294 item 7) on the owner's
revised starting point, then tune it, so that a Guild can live under `v1.2.1`'s Fatigue and form. The
pass:

1. builds four rule changes first, each a story with neutral defaults where the rule allows: Plunder,
   the Wealth an Encounter yields on top of the Patron's reward; the Quest reward by Tier as a range;
   one Level curve for wages and signing fees, reading a Hero's Calibre; and Loot drop rates by Tier
   and band;
2. adds the content Plunder needs: Plunder on 17 of today's 29 Encounters, 4 new Encounters and 2 new
   Quest templates where Quests rarely meet coin;
3. lands the owner's revised starting point (§2a) as one package, measured by one full Gate against
   `v1.2.3`'s close;
4. then tunes from it one change at a time to the bands the owner approves (§2): Quests a Year and
   the Roster first, then the player's solvency, the Rivals', the Market and the Contracts' figures;
5. then tunes Fatigue to its bands, the work `v1.2.1`-S11 stopped (DL-292).

The player stays solvent in most games. The Rivals fail fewer Quests, recruit, and fold within DL-83's
band. Games reach 20 years, so that the save is measured there again. #51 closes with it. The
replanning is DL-328, the owner's answers in §6.

**Aims at:** no new sentence; it restores the ground the design's sentences stand on. The owner's
playtest is not part of this release (the owner, Q42).

**Sessions:** 21–25, plus the sweeps' wall clock, about 25 minutes a full `gate` (agent's estimate) ·
**Depends on:** `v1.2.3` released, its closing Gate the baseline · **Implements:** #51 items 1 and 4;
DL-292's economy release; DL-290 item 5's bands, once approved; DL-328.

**Gate:**
- *Correct and fast.* At the close, a full `gate` alone meets every line of `harness/gate.json`,
  the save after 20 years included, now that games reach it. The estimate's two limits hold.
- *The bands* the owner approved at S8 are met at the close, or each one missed is explained and

### v1.2.4-S1 · Plunder, the rule · 2–2.5 sessions · opus

- *The consequence.* `{ kind: "plunder", weight, line }` on a Degree's list, in the closed
  Consequence union (`engine/src/content-types.ts`) and the Encounters' kind
  (`content/src/kinds/encounters.ts`). The `wealth` consequence leaves both. `weight` is a finite
  number above 0; `line` is optional text under the page's words rules (`docs/agents/ui-text.md`).
  - *On Success or Triumph,* a gain: `round(weight × the Tier's plunderGain)`.
  - *On Failure,* a possible loss: with chance `plunderLossChance`, `round(weight × the Tier's
    plunderLoss)`; otherwise nothing.
  - *A Setback* uses Failure's list unless it has its own, as consequences already do
    (`resolve.ts`, the Degree's fallback).
  - Negotiation does not touch it: the Patron does not pay it.
- *The settings.* `questTiers.tiers[].plunderGain` and `plunderLoss`, Silver at a weight of 1, 0
  here and set at S7; `plunderLossChance` 0.15. The loss's roll draws on a stream of its own, so that
  no other draw moves.
- *The reward.* The Patron pays the Quest's reward as agreed at dispatch (× Negotiation, as today)
  on any band but `fail`; the reward factor the `wealth` consequence multiplied
  (`working.wealthFactor`, `resolve.ts`) is removed.
- *The money.* Each Encounter's result carries its Plunder, signed whole Silver, and the line it
  used; `quest.encounter` carries both, and `quest.resolved` carries the reward and the Plunder
  apart. The Guild is credited once, as the Quest concludes (`phases/quests-conclude.ts`), with the
  reward and the Plunder, which it keeps whatever the band (Q15). The save's validator checks both
  are whole Silver.
- *The words* (`words/src/narrate.ts`). An Encounter's sentence gives its Plunder and its line; the
  Quest's result reads "reward +x, Plunder +y, Fame +z". A Plunder consequence without a line takes a
  fallback by the Encounter's kind and Plunder's sign, two or three a pair, chosen from the Quest so
  that the same Quest always reads the same; the set grows with future content (Q24).
- *The page.* The Quest report's Encounter rows give each one's Plunder and line; its Rewards give
  the reward and the Plunder as two chips. The Treasury books Plunder by its sign, as DL-306 split
  Other: the Income line "Plunder" and the Expense line "Plunder lost" (`web/src/state/treasury.ts`,
  whose readers are exhaustive by event).
- *The CLI* narrates the same sentences (`cli/src/advance.ts`).
- *The harness.* A counter of its own, Plunder gained and lost by side and Tier, beside the Quest
  rewards (`harness/src/counters.ts`, `game-counters.ts`), which then count the Patron's reward
  alone; the report's "Taken" rows and its share line.
- *Choosing Quests* reads the Patron's reward alone: the Board, the dispatch preview, the Rivals'
  choices and the player policy estimate no Plunder (Q25).
- *The editor.* `npm run edit -- encounters consequence add|change|remove` take the `plunder` kind,
  its weight and line; `quests doctor` checks them.
- *The content.* The 8 Encounters that carry `wealth` today (a-word-with-the-fence, old-records,
  the-flooded-ford, the-hungry-ward, the-patrons-steward, the-strongroom, the-trapped-passage,
  the-wardens-seal) carry `plunder` in its place, their weights and lines drafted and written fresh,
  final at S2's review (Q38).
- *The docs.* CONTEXT's Plunder (Q26), the Encounter's list of consequences and the Quest report;
  `docs/agents/quests.md`; the `encounter-draft` skill's consequences step; `docs/data-model.md`.

**Acceptance:**

### v1.2.4-S5 · The Level curve and Calibre · 1 session · sonnet, opus verify

- *Calibre* (Q20, Q33). `heroCalibre(hero, content)`, the one number a wage and a signing fee read:
  today the Hero's Stat sum (`heroStatSum`). The future work on Potential changes this one function.
  CONTEXT's Calibre: "A Hero's single measure of ability that their wage and signing fee read; today
  the sum of their Stats (DL-328). _Avoid_: Ability, Score, Rating."
- *The curve, one rule for both* (`engine/src/contract.ts`, `selectors.ts`'s signing fee):
  - The wage: `floor((wageBase + wagePerLevel × Level + wageLevelCurve × Level ^ wageLevelExponent +

  - The signing fee: `floor((signingFeeBase + signingFeePerLevel × Level + signingFeeLevelCurve ×
    Level ^ signingFeeLevelExponent + Calibre ÷ signingFeeCalibreDivisor) × (1 − Negotiation))`.
  - The Level's part is one helper both read.
- *The settings.* `wageStatDivisor` and `signingFeeStatDivisor` renamed `wageCalibreDivisor` and
  `signingFeeCalibreDivisor`; the curves 0 and the exponents 1, so the quick run's result must be

## 4. Risks

- *Memory.* A full `gate` holds about 1.3 GB a game process, 8 at once; a `v1.2.1` trial was stopped
  by the system for low memory (DL-292). Trials run one at a time, nothing else alongside.
- *Wall clock.* A full `gate` that plays 20 years took about 25 minutes at `v1.2.0`'s close. From S7
  the plan holds about 25 to 30 of them, about 12 hours of runs.
- *S7 reads rich.* Until S9, the player keeps a Roster of 8 and about 130 Quests a Year, against the
  starting point's 12 and 60; at 130 Quests the revised rewards leave the player about +900g a Year
  ahead (the model). S7's Gate is a baseline, not a verdict.
- *Levels and wages pull together.* Fewer, longer Quests slow the Levels, and the wages and fees
  follow the Level: S9 holds the Level bands with `xpFactor` before S10 tunes the curve.
- *Content moves play.* S3's Encounters join every matching Beat, and its templates change their
  Spheres' draws; S6's tables bring Loot to 9 templates. Each is reported by its quick run before S7.
- *A windfall.* A Unique Item at 50,000 sells for 37,500, about 46% of a middle Year's Quest income
  at the starting point; a folded Rival's Unique Items sell at their whole value.
- *Levers that pull against each other.* The player's rewards are the Rivals' lost Quests, and the
  Rivals' Wealth sets the Transfer market. S10 and S11 each re-read the other's Targets.
- *A band that no setting reaches* is reported with its trials, and the owner chooses: a rule change,
  a new band, or the miss accepted (§6).
- *The save.* Games that reach 20 years bring the save back to about 17 MiB gzipped. The Gate's mean
  under 30 MiB holds unless the Rosters grow again (R1); the player's Roster now grows by design
  (S9).
