# v1.1.5 — The harness's player choosing by chance, one game's memory, the Board's unavailable Heroes last, the Roster as a Table, and v1.1.4's open questions answered

**Objective:** the owner's answers of 2026-10-03 to what v1.1.4 left open (DL-168), and two features
of the page the owner added the same day (DL-169), as one v1.1.x patch. v1.1.4 shipped with 13 of 50
harness Guilds bankrupt, most after their ninth year, and the DL-83 targets missed (DL-166, DL-167).
Before any rule of the economy changes, the owner wants the harness's automatic player to play
better, and each play change measured on its own:
- *One game's memory.* A 20-year game's save of about 120 MB grows its process to about 1.7 GB, which
  holds a sweep to about 8 processes on a 27.6 GB machine. Its main cause is found and fixed, with the
  games playing the same.
- *The harness's player choosing by chance.* It sends its Parties to the Quests they are likeliest to
  win, not to the highest reward it can staff. A new 50 × 20 baseline measures it.
- *Two play changes, each with its own sweep.* An Injury falls on the next-weakest unhurt Hero when
  the weakest is already hurt (E3). The four positive earned Traits go back to a chance of 0.05, for
  about 1.5 Traits a Hero career.
- *Small fixes.* `partyFor` adds a Hero only for a real gain (R1). The estimate's harness test gains a
  check with Morale's consequences in (E2). The words of the Earned Trait, the Chance band and the
  configuration's comments follow the owner's answers.
- *A trial, not shipped.* One 50 × 20 sweep with the XP split across the Party, for the owner's
  decision on DL-148 item 19.
- *Two features of the page* (DL-169). On the Board, the Heroes who cannot go, Away or Injured, are
  listed last in the Party builder, their chips reading just "Away" and "Injured". The Roster switches
  between Tiles and a Table of each Hero's effective Stats, its columns chosen, sorted by any of
  them, and shows each Hero's Traits and Items.

At the close, the owner's rule decides what follows (DL-168 item 16): v1.2's Random Events, or an
economy pass first as `v1.1.6` (#51).

**Sessions:** 10–12 · **Depends on:** `v1.1.4` released (pull request #50) · **Implements:** DL-168,
DL-169.

## 4. Risks

- *The memory's cause needs the save changed.* If one game's memory is mostly the save's own size,
  no fix keeps the fingerprints. S1 then stops and reports, and #34's historical set, with its
  schema change, is the owner's to bring forward.
- *The new player is slower.* Choosing by chance estimates each Quest left after each Party. S2's
  probe measures the cost before S3, and S2 may estimate once a choice.
- *Fifty seeds are noisy.* A count of Bankruptcies holds to about ±3 in 50 games (DL-166), and the
  rule's band is 3 to 7. A count near either edge is reported as such, with S3's beside it.
- *S6 changes a game.* A gain of exactly the margin is rare but possible. The fingerprint sweep after
  it finds it, and its DL entry measures it alone.
- *The fingerprint sweep differs* (DL-169 item 15). S1 and S6 in one sweep save one, but a difference
  needs a second, at S1's commit, to say whose it is: about 22 minutes more, and the saving is then
  about 6 minutes, not 28.
- *Fewer replays* (DL-169 item 16). A nondeterminism brought in by S4 or S5 would surface only at the
  close's replay, and cost their sweeps again. Each story's `npm test` guards it: `play.test.ts`
  plays a seed twice and compares the saves byte for byte, `sweep.test.ts` replays a seed after a
  sweep, and S4's tests hold its draws as a twin's before the change.
- *The Traits' aim.* At 0.05, v1.1.4's policy earned 1.6 to 1.9 a career; the new player and S4 may
  move it. S5 reports outside 1.25 to 1.75 and changes nothing on its own.
- *The baseline moves twice.* The M8 baseline DL-83 measures against moved with `partyFor`'s Party
  (v1.1.4-S6) and moves again with the choice of Quests. S3's DL entry records the policy beside the
  numbers.
- *The script's room.* It is 800,111 bytes against 850 kB: about 50 kB for S11 and S12. A Table, a
  panel and tabs may pass it; the owner's 900 kB is then taken (DL-169 item 12), and recorded.
- *The Roster on a phone.* Twenty Stats and seven columns of the Hero's do not fit 360px. The five tabs
  and the Table's own scrolling box carry it; S12's layout specs hold 44px targets, no page overflow
  and the contrast of green and red in light and dark.
