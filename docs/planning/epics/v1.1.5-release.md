# v1.1.5 — The harness's player choosing by chance, one game's memory, and v1.1.4's open questions answered

**Objective:** the owner's answers of 2026-10-03 to what v1.1.4 left open (DL-168), as one v1.1.x
patch. v1.1.4 shipped with 13 of 50 harness Guilds bankrupt, most after their ninth year, and the
DL-83 targets missed (DL-166, DL-167). Before any rule of the economy changes, the owner wants the
harness's automatic player to play better, and each play change measured on its own:
- *One game's memory.* A 20-year game's save of about 120 MB grows its process to about 1.7 GB, which
  holds a sweep to about 8 processes on a 27.6 GB machine. Its main cause is found and fixed, with the
  games playing the same.
- *The harness's player choosing by chance.* It sends its Parties to the Quests they are likeliest to
  win, not to the highest reward it can staff. A new 50 × 20 baseline measures it.
- *Two play changes, each with its own sweep.* An Injury falls on the next-weakest unhurt Hero when
  the weakest is already hurt (E3). The four positive earned Traits go back to a chance of 0.05, for
  about 1.5 Traits a Hero career.
- *Small fixes.* `partyFor` adds a Hero only for a real gain (R1). The estimate's harness test gains a
  check with Morale's consequences in (E2). The words of the Earned Trait, the Chance band and the
  configuration's comments follow the owner's answers.
- *A trial, not shipped.* One 50 × 20 sweep with the XP split across the Party, for the owner's
  decision on DL-148 item 19.

At the close, the owner's rule decides what follows (DL-168 item 16): v1.2's Random Events, or an
economy pass first as `v1.1.6` (#51).

**Sessions:** 8–10 · **Depends on:** `v1.1.4` released (pull request #50) · **Implements:** DL-168.

**Gate:**

- *S6 changes a game.* A gain of exactly the margin is rare but possible. Its sweep before S2 finds
  it, and its DL entry measures it alone.

  move it. S5 reports outside 1.25 to 1.75 and changes nothing on its own.
- *The baseline moves twice.* The M8 baseline DL-83 measures against moved with `partyFor`'s Party
  (v1.1.4-S6) and moves again with the choice of Quests. S3's DL entry records the policy beside the
  numbers.
