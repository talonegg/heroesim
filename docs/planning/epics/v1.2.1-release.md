# v1.2.1 — Fatigue and the Captain: a Guild that must rotate its Heroes, and one who leads them

**Objective:** make sending a Hero cost something beyond the Quest. Fatigue rises each Day a Hero is
away and falls at home, and a tired Hero brings less of their Physical and Magical Stats, and less of
their Mental and Social, to every Check, and is injured and killed more often. Morale's form becomes a
continuous line around the baseline, and an Injury taken on a Quest weighs on the rest of it, unless a
Healer or a heal Item relieves it. Each Guild names one Captain, whose Party and Roster gain from their
Leadership. Every Guild, the player's and the Rivals', rests its tired Heroes by a band. Every figure
is a setting, so later releases tune it without code.

**Aims at** (the [Guild Management Design](../../management-design.md)'s sentences, Q7 b):
- "I can't send her; she's exhausted." Fatigue's cost is seen on the Board's chance and the Party
  builder before the Party sets out.
- "I need to give him a chance." Rotation sends the fresher, weaker Heroes out, which v1.2.0's
  structure alone did not (DL-276).

The owner's answers to Q1–Q17 (§6) set the scope, and the architecture review's adopted candidates
(§1, *How it is built*; §7) shape the code; DL-280 records both.

## 1. Stories

Opus builds every story but S1, S9's views and S10's counters, and does every verification.

**How it is built** (the architecture review of 2026-10-11, [its report](./v1.2.1-architecture.html),
summarised in §7; its candidates adopted where marked). A story that builds a candidate cites it, and
its acceptance holds the review's two rules: the new module is tested through its own interface, and
the path it replaces is removed, not left beside it.
- *The form module* (candidate 1). Form stops being one number a Hero carries into a Check. One module
  works out each member's formed Stats on an Encounter, from Morale's factor, Fatigue's by area, the
  hurt factor by area after relief and the Captain's factor, in `queryParty`, the one place members are
  built. `statOf` reads the formed value; the operators' signatures stay. `formDecided` compares the
  formed roll with the raw one.
- *The Quest clock* (candidate 2). A pure module gives the Day an Encounter falls on and a Hero's
  Fatigue on it, from the Fatigue the Party records at dispatch. The resolver and the estimate both
  read it; the resolver no longer reads end-of-Quest State for it.
- *The Party's pool* (candidate 3). The Best Party module gains `partyPool(world, guild, deps)`: the
  available Heroes less those a rest band keeps home. The Rival, the harness's policy and the Board use
  it; `partyMarginOf` becomes one reader of any per-Guild setting.
- *Consumables in the engine* (candidate 4). What to buy and what to carry move from the harness into
  the engine beside `gearPurchase`, shared by the policy and the Rivals.
- *Roles in content* (candidate 5). The engine names no Class and no Stat (ADR-0006): a Class may be
  marked to relieve the hurt, and one Stat marked as the captaincy's, each checked by the content kinds.
- *Bands* (candidate 6), as Fatigue's lands: one band function and an at-or-past comparison, Morale's,
  Regard's, the chance's and Fatigue's readers over it.
- *The Roster's columns* (candidate 7), to the extent S9 needs: a group says what kind it is, so a State
  group is no Stat area; the form line's words move to `words/`.
- *Not adopted:* one Encounter step shared by the estimate and the resolver (candidate 8). S4 adds a
  parity test over fixed Encounters instead, and the estimate keeps its no-one-falls assumption.

## 4. Risks

- *Morale's wider spread.* Its factor runs 0.75 to 1.25, against 0.93 to 1.06; with Fatigue and a hurt
  factor of 0.6, a Hero can bring 0.42 of a Stat. Every Target moves; they are reported. The estimate's
  Morale-in line may move: it goes to the owner if it misses.
- *Speed.* The estimate builds a Party a distinct Day, and the Best Party searches with it. S4's quick
  run and the speed run watch `history.speed` and `validate.speed`.
- *One purchase an afternoon.* A Rival's heal Items compete with its building, gear and recruiting;
  S8's boxes show whether its Hall or Roster slows.
- *Draws moved.* A Rival's new choices draw from its stream (ADR-0009), so pinned Rival counts in the
  harness's boxes move; tests pinned to content are made independent of it, not re-pinned.
- *The bundle.* 4.3 kB of headroom before S9; the budget rises first.
- *Size.* 15–18 sessions. S8 and the Party builder's relief line are the most separable.

## 5. What the plan rests on

Facts checked on `29c90c7`, 2026-10-11:
- Form is one number a member carries, set in `queryParty` and applied in the operators' `statOf`;
  the estimate shares the capability but not the roll's spread, the reroll or the kit
  (`engine/src/quest-engine/`).
- The resolver runs on the return night, before the Hero State Engine; nothing in the Quest Engine
  knows Days (`engine/src/pipeline.ts`).
- Heal Items are spent after the sequence, longest Injury first (`consumables.ts`); Rivals buy only
  wearables and carry nothing (`engine/src/gear.ts`, `controllers/rival.ts`).
- The Best Party module is one, but each caller builds its pool with `availableHeroes`, which reads no
  config (`engine/src/best-party.ts`).
- No Fatigue or Captain exists in the engine, content or glossary; the Synergy "Under a captain" does.
- v1.2.0's close: 8 of 17 Targets outside their bands, the player's Deaths 0.24 a game, strong
  successes 85.2%, failed Quests 1.1%, the bundle 845,738 bytes (DL-274, DL-273).
