# v1.1.7 — v1.1.6's open items, a calendar, Class icons, Roster filters and a History of those hired

**Objective:** the owner's answers of 2026-10-06 (DL-206) to v1.1.6's open items and to four features
the owner asked for, as one v1.1.x patch. The open items were walked through one at a time: the
weights a new Guild's deals and a Guildmaster's sex ignored (DL-202), the "why it stopped" sentences
written twice (DL-198), and every judgement call DL-205 listed from DL-187 to DL-205. The features
were grilled one question at a time:
- *A History of those hired.* A Hero who passes through the Unattached pool and is never hired leaves
  no entry in the History; their row is already deleted when they leave (DL-27).
- *Class icons.* A small icon for each Class before a Hero's name wherever a Hero is listed with
  their Class, chosen by a new `icon` field on the Class in content.
- *Roster filters.* The Roster filtered by Class and by whether a Hero is at the Hall, injured or
  away, through one filter element, several choices to a filter, that every filter on the page uses.
- *A calendar.* Every date a player reads is "Year Y Day D": the Year from 1, the Day from 1 to 360,
  Day 361 being Day 1 of the next Year; the time of day after it where it is shown.

No play changes: every game plays as before, though S2, S5 and S6 move the fingerprints (a new
setting, the History's pool entries, the content version) and prove it by `compare`.

### v1.1.7-S2 · Weighted deals at creation and a Guildmaster's sex · 1 session · opus

  Classes) and the Patrons' Spheres at creation (`create.ts`, `dealEvenly` over the Spheres) read the
  weights (DL-202): when every row's weight is 1, which is every shipped row's, the deal is
  `dealEvenly`'s, its draws the same in number and order, so every game is as before; when any

  engine's `sexes` exactly, each 0 or more, one above 0), drawn by `weightedPick` over `sexes` where
  `pick` drew (`generators/guilds.ts`'s `drawGuildmaster`): with 1 and 1 the same draw (DL-202's
  proof). The player's chosen sex still replaces the drawn one after the draw (M9-S2).
- *The fingerprint.* The new setting is in every save's `meta.config` and `configVersion` rises, so
  the `neutral` fingerprint moves; the 4 × 2 quick check with the setting taken out, its read given

### v1.1.7-S5 · No History entry for a Hero never hired · 1.5–2 sessions · opus

  `hero.left-pool` are still emitted, so the Turn's log, the "new in the Unattached pool" stop and
  the harness see them, but are not appended to the History. A Hero dealt onto a Roster at creation

  recorded (item 21). The row's deletion as they leave (`pool-refresh.ts`, DL-27) reads "named by no
  entry" in place of "named only passively".

  `docs/data-model.md` rules 8 and 10 and §7, and ADR-0008 gain dated amendments: the pool's comings
  and goings of a Hero never hired are not History.
- *What reads the History.* The harness's Hero counters (`observeHeroes`, `counters.ts`) and its
  per-type marks (`play.ts`) read the Turn's events for the pool, so its counters are the same; the
  validator's `removedHeroInHistory` and the page's History search need nothing new.

- *The fingerprint* moves; a full `gate` measures the save's gzipped mean and `compare` against
