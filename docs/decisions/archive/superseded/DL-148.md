# DL-148: superseded items

What later entries superseded of DL-148 (DL-215). It is in [DL-148-168-v1.1.4](../DL-148-168-v1.1.4.md).
There, an item superseded in full is a stub, and its text is below. An item superseded in part keeps
its text there, with a note, and below is what superseded it.

## DL-148 item 3 — in part, by DL-181

**Superseded in part by** DL-181, 2026-10-04: The order: the economy waits for Random Events "as DL-83 item 1 orders"; the economy pass first, as v1.1.6.

> As DL-168 item 16 says, this supersedes DL-83 item 1's and DL-148 item 3's order, the economy after Random Events.

## DL-148 item 8 — in part, by DL-168

**Superseded in part by** DL-168 item 9, 2026-10-03: The Degrees' first values, triumph 1.25 and "at least half"; now checkDegrees.triumph 1.35 and strongTriumphShare 0.6.

> `checkDegrees.triumph` 1.35 and `strongTriumphShare` 0.6 are accepted as the design values. They supersede DL-148 item 8's first values, 1.25 and "at least half".

## DL-148 item 9 — in part, by DL-283

**Superseded in part by** DL-283 item 1, 2026-10-11: Each Hero's own Morale band's factor; now Morale's factor, a line through `moraleBaseline` at their own Morale.

> **The line.** `moraleFactor(morale, config)` in `quest-engine/form.ts`: 1 at `moraleBaseline`, a quarter more half the scale above it and a quarter less half the scale below, the same for every Guild.

## DL-148 item 10 — in part, by DL-168

**Superseded in part by** DL-168 item 9, 2026-10-03: `hurtCheckFactor` 0.85; now 0.93, provisional.

> `moraleCheckFactor` (wretched 0.93, low 0.97, steady 1, high 1.03, elated 1.06) and `hurtCheckFactor` 0.93 supersede item 10's 0.85 and S3's first values as provisional values

## DL-148 item 10 — in part, by DL-284

**Superseded in part by** DL-284 items 1 and 2, 2026-10-11: The hurt one factor on all a Hero adds; now `hurtCheckFactor` on their Physical Stats and 1 − (1 − it) ÷ 2 on the rest.

> **The form module**, `quest-engine/form.ts`: `formedStats(stats, { morale, fatigue, hurt }, deps)`
> is each Stat times Morale's factor, Fatigue's by its area (1 − FS·f ÷ `fatigueMax` on the Physical
> and Magical, half that on the Mental and Social) and the hurt's by its area (`hurtCheckFactor` on
> the Physical, 1 − (1 − it) ÷ 2 on the rest), multiplied; the area is content's.
