# DL-50: superseded items

What later entries superseded of DL-50 (DL-215). It is in [DL-001-085-first-build](../DL-001-085-first-build.md).
There, an item superseded in full is a stub, and its text is below. An item superseded in part keeps
its text there, with a note, and below is what superseded it.

## DL-50 item 3 — in part, by DL-285

**Superseded in part by** DL-285 item 3, 2026-10-11: `resolve.ts` asking the kit "for the heals after the sequence"; now a heal is asked for at each Injury as it lands.

> **A heal Item at the Injury:** the kit reaches the Injury's step (`Applying.kit`); as an Injury
> lands, the infirmary applied, `kit.heal(injury, index)` spends the heal definition healing most
> Days (ties by key), one an Injury while any is left, shortening it to no fewer than 1 Day, which
> the return applies, and the Hero joins `OnQuest.relieved` for the rest of the Quest. Spent on a
> Hero who then dies, it is lost. `item.expended` for a heal names its Encounter, `encounterIndex`.

The text, as the entry held it:

6. **`heal`.** After the sequence, when the Injuries are final (a Hero who died has none), the heal
   Items carried are given out one at a time, the definition healing most Days first (ties by key),
   each to **the Injury with the most Days** among those not yet treated (ties to the one taken
   first), shortening it by the definition's `days` **to no fewer than 1 Day** — the Injury is
   shortened, not cured, so `hero.injured`, its night and any Trait it leaves on healing still
   follow (DL-29). The Days shortened are those after the infirmary (DL-29 item 8). **One Item per
   Injury**; an Injury at 1 Day takes none; Items left over go home. No draw.
