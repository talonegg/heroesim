## Fatigue and the Captain (DL-290)

Fatigue's lines (DL-290), the player's Guild and the Rivals apart. A Party is read as it set out: its Heroes' `fatigueAtDispatch` by `fatigueBand`, and whether its Guild's Captain is among them. The pool is sampled once a Day in the world each Guild sends its Parties from, the player's on a night's Turn, before the policy sends the Heroes the night brought home, and a Rival's on a morning's, which its afternoon reads: the free Heroes (`availableHeroes`) and those `partyPool` leaves out. A recovery runs from a Hero's homecoming, alive, in a band at or past the one it ends below, to the night a crossing takes their Fatigue below it, or is cut short when they are sent again or leave every Roster; its Days are counted from the return.
